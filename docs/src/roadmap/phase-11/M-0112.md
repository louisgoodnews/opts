## 🎯 Objective

Public transport runs by timetable: a scheduled vehicle spawns at the start of its route at the right time, follows the lane graph through its stops with the timetable (arrival, dwell, departure), and despawns at the end of its route.

**What exists already (build on it, do not duplicate):**

- [M-0049](../phase-4/M-0049.md) explicitly excludes scheduled traffic; [M-0095](../phase-4/M-0095.md) excludes 'where and when vehicles spawn' and notes that articulated buses and trains sit in groups left at density 0 by `unsched_vehgroups.txt`, i.e. they belong to scheduled traffic. Chains with the kinematic trailer follower already work ([AI vehicle configuration](../../architecture/ai-vehicle-config.md)).
- [M-0099](../phase-4/M-0099.md) (not started) delivers the `.hof` parser, timetable model, `.ttl/.ttp/.ttr` and bus stop data, route callbacks; [M-0098](../phase-4/M-0098.md) (not started) one script state per consist.
- Gap in M-0099 for this milestone: it models the timetable for the player's vehicle and for scripts, and states 'scheduled AI buses (M-0049 phase 2)' out of scope; it does not specify a timetable reader API for non-player vehicles (list of trips by start time on a route, per trip stop times) nor the data for which route a map's `.ttp`/`.ttr` assigns to which AI group. This milestone adds that API on top of the M-0099 model.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** a scheduler reading all trips of the active map's timetables for the session date (weekday/holiday validity as the data defines it); spawn at the first stop of a trip at its start time (or at session start in the right place when the session starts mid-day); route following over the lane graph and rail lanes for trams/trains; stop handling (stop at the bus stop position, dwell for the timetable, indicator and door variables to the vehicle script); despawn at the last stop; the vehicle's own IBIS/destination display fed from M-0099; settings for density of scheduled traffic.

**Out of scope:** passenger boarding (M-0101), signalling for trains, switch handling (M-0113), timetable editing, inter-line transfers, depot rosters beyond the first and last trip.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. For a map with a published timetable, a headless run over one hour spawns every trip whose start time falls in the hour (100 % of the scheduled starts, within 5 s of the timetable).
2. A spawned bus arrives at each stop within 30 s of the timetable when unobstructed, dwells at least the timetable dwell, and departs not before the scheduled time; the log lists the stops of one trip with times.
3. At the end of its route the vehicle despawns within 10 s of reaching the last stop and no vehicle remains from the finished trip (entity count test).
4. A session started at 12:00 shows the vehicles that would be en route at that time at plausible positions (within the route polyline, between the correct stops), not only vehicles starting after 12:00.
5. An articulated bus or tram trip works with its trailer chain (reuses M-0095 chains); destination and line strings reach the vehicle's script variables.

All gates pass for milestones that change code (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

## ✍️ Closing notes (2026-10-23)

Built: the reader of the timetable (`active_runs`, weekday by file name), scheduled road vehicles and rail vehicles (spawn at a stop, between stops or from upstream, route over the lane graph or the tracks, stop control with dwell and scheduled departure, removal 8 s after the last stop when out of sight), the vehicles' own scripts for doors and destination display (`docs/src/architecture/scheduled-traffic.md`), the player's schedule (stop detection, delay, status bar line), the route and the scheduled vehicles on both maps, `traffic.scheduled`.

Measured at Ahlheim Hauptbahnhof, clock rate 1: arrivals 7 to 20 s early or up to 15 s late (S8 at the Hbf platform 14 s late); vehicles that start between two stops arrive early by up to 90 s, because their position is placed by the fraction of the leg and they drive at the traffic's pace. A run with the clock at 20 times the real rate drives its vehicles in real time, so delays grow there (it is a test of the cap, 25 vehicles, not of punctuality). Doors: a Mercedes articulated bus opened and closed both door pairs within the dwell.

Limits, stated plainly:

1. **Criterion 1 (every start within 5 s of the timetable) is not met and cannot be:** vehicles exist only near the player (40 to 450 m), by design, because only the loaded tiles have lanes; the headless day run is the accelerated run above.
2. **Passengers** do not walk to or from AI vehicles (M-0101 owns boarding; AI vehicles have no passenger system); the doors open and close for the time the passengers would take.
3. **Trains and trams** run their units' scripts and the doors protocol; the BR 425/426 models have no door animation, the Tram map's tours are invisible ghost vehicles (`NF6D_KI`), so no scheduled tram with doors was seen.
4. The destination text was verified as variables (line text `156`, terminus row 113 reached `target_index_int`), not as pixels on a bus: my attempts to put a camera next to a stopped bus did not frame one.
5. Tours of school holidays (`Ferien`) are not run (no school calendar) and public holidays are unknown.
