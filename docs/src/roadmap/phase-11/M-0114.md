## 🎯 Objective

Streaming adapts to how fast the player moves: at speed, whole tiles are loaded ahead along the direction of travel (tile-level); when slow or stopped, objects near the player are loaded in detail first (object-level), so a stationary player sees a complete near field and a fast one never outruns the world.

**What exists already (build on it, do not duplicate):**

- Tile streaming exists ([map streaming](../../architecture/map-streaming.md)): a ring of resident tiles around the player, async load jobs, the Mercator tile grid with exact seams ([streaming coordinates](../../architecture/streaming-coordinates.md), anchor re-centring deferred), terrain carving and per-tile overlays.
- [M-0088](../phase-9/M-0088.md) established the rule that no blocking work runs on the main thread; any streaming change keeps it.
- No speed dependence exists: the ring radius and load order do not depend on velocity; this is the new part.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** measure the present behaviour (load time per tile, time to a complete near field after a teleport, pop-in at 90 km/h); a streaming policy with two regimes and a smooth blend by speed (tile-level lookahead cone along the heading at speed; near-first object ordering when slow); budgets for loads per frame; settings and a debug overlay; relation to anchor re-centring (streaming-coordinates 2.3).

**Out of scope:** a new tile format, LOD or impostor generation, changing the tile grid, background download, multi-threaded decoding beyond what exists.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. Baselines are recorded in the design page (time to complete near field, pop-in distance at 90 km/h, main-thread time per frame for streaming).
2. At 90 km/h the first not-yet-loaded tile ahead is at least 1.5 tile widths away from the vehicle in 95 % of frames in a 120 s drive (test or recorded run); baseline value is stated for comparison.
3. After a teleport (or stop) the objects within 50 m are resident within 2 s, 25 % faster than the baseline or equal if the baseline already meets it.
4. The main-thread time attributable to streaming stays below 2 ms per frame in the same drive and no blocking wait is introduced (M-0088 rule).
5. `streaming.mode` forced to `tile` or `object` reproduces the respective regime and is shown in the debug overlay.

All gates pass for milestones that change code (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Policy thrash at the speed threshold | Blend continuously, hysteresis on regime changes |
| Higher memory in tile-level mode | Hard cap on resident tiles; eviction by distance behind the vehicle |
| Seam artifacts when loading order changes | Seams depend on grid maths, not order; regression test on neighbour carving |
