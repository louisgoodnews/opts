## 🎯 Objective

AI vehicles behave like careful drivers: they recognise and brake for obstacles (a stopped bus, a closed level-crossing barrier), change lanes conservatively with signal, gap, change and resume, and obey traffic lights with a rule-breaking tendency from `[rowdy_factor]` at yellow and red. The first deliverable is an audit; only the gaps it finds are closed.

**What exists already (build on it, do not duplicate):**

- Baseline ([AI traffic](../../architecture/ai-traffic.md) status notes, [M-0049](../phase-4/M-0049.md), [M-0095](../phase-4/M-0095.md)): IDM car following with a lookahead plan (150 m planned, 100 m leader/light/conflict lookahead), per-vehicle profiles from the vehicle files (`AiProfile`, `[rowdy_factor]` already shifts desired speed: calmest vehicles at 90 % of the open-road speed), traffic lights (stop 1 m before the line on red and red-yellow, on yellow when braking at <= 3 m/s2 suffices, approach distance), right of way and conflict zones, MOBIL lane changes with indicator (3 s), target-lane priority, gap and follower-safety checks (target follower may brake at most 1.5 m/s2), strategic changes, and the 2026-10-17 kinematics ([AI traffic kinematics](../../architecture/ai-traffic-kinematics.md): front/rear axle bodies, curve speed limits, arcs).
- Recorded open points: Q-AI05 (how AI drivers treat yellow, `[traffic_light_stop]`/`[traffic_light_jump]`), Q-AI11 (OMSI lane-change behaviour), Q-AIC-002 (meaning of `[rowdy_factor]`), `[traffic_light_stop]` has no effect yet, and level-crossing barriers/stopped buses as obstacles are not described anywhere in the AI docs.
- Calibration against OMSI stays in [pending OMSI verification](../../architecture/pending-omsi-verification.md).

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** Phase A audit (a written gap list from code, tests and a headless run); Phase B obstacle recognition and braking (any standing vehicle in the lane ahead including stopped buses at stops, closed level-crossing barriers as standing obstacles at the stop line); Phase C conservative lane change as a state machine (signal, gap check, change, resume speed) tuned for gentleness; Phase D traffic-light compliance with `[rowdy_factor]` governing the probability of passing yellow and rarely red, with documented bounds.

**Out of scope:** scheduled buses and trams (M-0112), rail switches (M-0113), pedestrians, collisions with the player, signalling for trains, tuning against a running OMSI (Q-AI01).

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

### Phase A: Audit
- [ ] Read the code paths in `opts-traffic` (`lanechange`, `signals`, `conflict`, `rightofway`, IDM leader selection) and `traffic*.rs` in `opts-game`; write `docs/src/meta/ai-traffic-audit-<date>.md` with a table: behaviour wanted, present (test or log evidence), absent, partial.
- [ ] Headless measurement on Berlin-Spandau and one more map: counts of hard brakes (> 3 m/s2), lane-change aborts, red-light violations, standing obstacles ignored.

### Phase B: Obstacles
- [ ] Standing vehicles (bus at stop, broken-down, queue end) are leaders even when not on the same lane id (overlap by lateral position); level-crossing barrier state from the object's signal/animation variable becomes a standing obstacle at its stop line (needs a data check of crossing objects first).
- [ ] Overtaking of a stopped vehicle only when allowed (`overtaking_prohib`, oncoming gap) else wait.

### Phase C: Lane change
- [ ] Make the sequence explicit: indicator on, gap evaluation continuous, change, resume desired speed; abort rules; never within a junction/zone window (as today).

### Phase D: Traffic lights
- [ ] Yellow decision: pass if stopping would need more than a comfortable deceleration; `[rowdy_factor]` raises the pass probability inside documented bounds; red running only above a high rowdy value and never into a conflict with a vehicle already moving.
- [ ] Open points Q-AI05/Q-AIC-002 stay marked; assumptions listed.

### Phase E: Verification
- [ ] Scenario tests with negative controls (the test fails when the new rule is disabled); headless runs with before/after numbers.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. The audit document exists and lists every wanted behaviour of the three areas as present, partial or absent with evidence; only 'partial' and 'absent' rows become work.
2. A vehicle approaching a stationary bus that blocks its lane stops behind it with a gap of 2-5 m and no deceleration beyond 4 m/s2; the same scenario fails when the new obstacle rule is switched off (negative control).
3. A level-crossing with the barrier down stops approaching AI vehicles before the stop line and releases them within 3 s of the barrier opening (scenario on a crossing object, or a synthetic one if the data check shows no suitable object).
4. Lane change: in 100 simulated changes on a two-lane road, 100 % show the indicator for at least 2 s before the move, the target follower brakes at most 1.5 m/s2 in 95 %, and speed recovers to 90 % of desired within 8 s after the change.
5. Traffic lights: with `[rowdy_factor]` 0 no vehicle that can stop passes red or yellow; with the maximum value the share of yellow passes rises monotonically; red violations stay below 1 % of arrivals on red over a 600 s run.

All gates pass for milestones that change code (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.
