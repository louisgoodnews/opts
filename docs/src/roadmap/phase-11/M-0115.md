## 🎯 Objective

The game knows when a vehicle or the camera is inside a tunnel (or another enclosed zone) and publishes this as a spatial zone that later features consume: sound reverb, ambient light in tunnels, weather shelter. This milestone delivers detection and the zone abstraction, not the consumers.

**What exists already (build on it, do not duplicate):**

- [Lighting system](../../architecture/lighting-system.md) section 4 documents light rooms: `[nomaplighting]`, a CPU light selection and a cabin layer for interior lights. Verified there: no scenery object declares an inside volume; tunnels have no special handling and are only recognisable by name (198 objects) and by 7 `[maplight]`/19 `[LightMapMapping]` entries.
- Sun and weather presently ignore enclosure: rain falls and the sun shines inside tunnels. The rooms work is about lamps lighting through walls, not about zones.
- Ground queries and tile data are available ([streaming coordinates](../../architecture/streaming-coordinates.md), terrain and object placement in `opts-game`).

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** a `SpatialZone` abstraction (kind `Tunnel`, id, bounds, enter/leave events) in the shared world; a detector with two strategies compared on the corpus: geometric (overhead ray casts against object meshes, roof coverage over a stretch of road) and tile-data (object classification by name/mesh class and `[LightMapMapping]`); a decision on which to ship; a debug overlay; a documented hook for sound reverb and light-room ambience.

**Out of scope:** reverb DSP and ambient light changes themselves (they consume the zone later; reverb is part of the audio work of M-0027/M-0102), rain shelter, indoor zones of buildings (platform halls, garages) beyond the tunnel kind.

### Implementation
- [ ] `SpatialZone` resource/component model in `opts-bevy` with `ZoneEntered`/`ZoneLeft` messages (event shape agreed with M-0118).
- [ ] Detector for the chosen strategy, evaluated at <= 10 Hz for the player vehicle and the camera; zones built at tile load, dropped at unload.
- [ ] Debug overlay and control-protocol `zones.query`.

### Hand-off
- [ ] Document the consumer contract (what reverb and light-room ambience read) in `lighting-system.md`; no consumer code.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. On the labelled set the chosen detector reaches at least 95 % recall and 95 % precision of tunnel-covered road length and the report shows the numbers for both strategies.
2. Driving into a tunnel on the reference map emits exactly one `ZoneEntered(Tunnel)` message within 0.2 s of the portal and one `ZoneLeft` at the exit; no flapping inside (test with a recorded drive).
3. Detection costs at most 0.2 ms per frame averaged over a 60 s drive; zones appear and disappear with tile streaming without leaks (entity count stable after load/unload cycles).
4. The consumer contract is written down and referenced from the lighting-system page.

All gates pass for milestones that change code (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tunnel meshes have no clean volume | Compare two strategies; keep a per-object override list for known exceptions |
| False zones under bridges and canopies | Require roof coverage over a minimum length (e.g. 15 m) and hysteresis on leave |
| Event shape changes with M-0118 | Order after M-0118 or code against an adapter |
