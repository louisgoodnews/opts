## 🎯 Objective

Weather changes how vehicles drive and what the player sees of them: wet roads lengthen braking, snow and ice lower grip, fog limits visibility for the player and for AI, and exhaust becomes visible in cold weather and under load.

**What exists already (build on it, do not duplicate):**

- The weather state exists: preset parameters and the shared `Wetness` resource written from `[groundwet]` (wetness, puddles, snow; [weather](../../architecture/weather.md) sections 6 and 9), fog and precipitation rendering, script variables. Nothing in `opts-sim` reads it: tyre friction is constant.
- [M-0073](../phase-10/M-0073.md) (planned) introduces the world physics with per-wheel contact; the friction coefficient must be a parameter of the contact, which this milestone feeds. If M-0073 is not merged, a grip multiplier on the current model is the interim hook.
- AI speed already depends on lane limits and curvature ([AI traffic kinematics](../../architecture/ai-traffic-kinematics.md)); a grip factor is a new input there.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** a `SurfaceGrip` model (dry/wet/snow/ice from `Wetness` and snow coverage, per road surface class) multiplying tyre friction; effect on braking distance, acceleration and cornering; the same factor reducing AI desired speed and increasing headway; fog visibility affecting AI following distance and the player's HUD hint; exhaust: a particle effect at the exhaust position, visible by temperature and engine load (cold start puffs, heavy acceleration), off in heat; settings for each effect.

**Out of scope:** tyre temperature or wear models, aquaplaning, hydraulic details of ABS, wind forces, real-weather sources (M-0116).

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. From 50 km/h with full braking, the stopping distance on wet ground is at least 20 % and on snow at least 100 % longer than dry for the same vehicle (test with documented coefficients and tolerance).
2. Changing the weather preset from dry to snow while driving changes the grip within 2 s without a discontinuity in vehicle state (no velocity jump above 0.1 m/s).
3. In snow the AI mean desired speed is lower than in dry by at least 10 % and no AI vehicle brakes harder than the reduced limit allows (headless run, comparison numbers recorded).
4. In fog the AI following distance rises according to visibility and the HUD shows a low-visibility hint below 100 m visibility.
5. With `graphics.exhaust` on, a cold-start bus at 5 degrees C shows a visible exhaust plume in a screenshot; at 30 degrees C and idle it does not; the effect costs at most 0.3 ms per frame with ten vehicles.

All gates pass for milestones that change code (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.
