## 🎯 Objective

OPTS does not compute a fare from the stop. Define the fare rule as a function of the stop, the trip and the ticket pack, so that the price a passenger pays depends on where they travel.

## 🧩 Scope

Owner decision 2026-11-22: kept separate from [M-0156](../phase-16/M-0156.md), which is not extended. This milestone is its own proposed item.

1. Write the fare rule as a short spec: the inputs (stop, trip, ticket pack) and the output (price). State which parts are verified and which come from the reading of the vehicle's fare scripts.
2. Settle the zone case from the scripts, in prose only.
3. Check the claim that prices rose across 1986 to 1994 against the base manual and the chronology control before it goes into any spec; until then it is marked unverified.

**Out of scope:** copying fare script text; using fare numbers from any other product ([ADR-0002](../../adr/0002-clean-room-policy.md)).

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. The fare rule spec exists, with its inputs and output, and links from [M-0156](../phase-16/M-0156.md).
2. A test computes a price for two stops on one trip and checks it against the spec.
3. The price-rise claim is either checked or marked unverified in the spec.
