## 🎯 Objective

The vision promises a single-player career with progression, and nothing in the roadmap plans it. Define and build the first slice: the state a career keeps, what ends a session, and what counts as progress.

## 🧩 Scope

Owner decision 2026-11-22: career and progression are in scope. This milestone proposes the first slice; the owner still confirms its scope.

1. One page defining the first slice: the state (money, owned vehicles, a shift), what ends a session, and what counts as progress.
2. The money part uses the cash-desk model of [M-0156](../phase-16/M-0156.md); there is one money model, not two.
3. Where the state is saved, it uses the save format owner's rules, not a separate store.

**Out of scope:** the economy numbers, vehicle prices and rules of any paid add-on. They are not a source for OPTS behaviour ([ADR-0002](../../adr/0002-clean-room-policy.md)).

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. The owner confirms this scope, or amends it, and the confirmation is recorded in [ISS-047](../../../dev/issues/ISS-047-career-and-progression.md).
2. The first-slice page exists and links to [M-0156](../phase-16/M-0156.md).
3. A session ends and a progress state is saved and reloaded (test).
