## 🎯 Objective

The first collision version pushes the vehicle out of static objects, AI vehicles and ground steps. It does not yet give a collision any consequence for the people in the bus or for the people outside it. This milestone adds the collision responses that go beyond the push: passengers who want to get off after a collision, and police and ambulance involvement.

## 🧩 Scope

Owner decision 2026-11-22: collisions with extra behaviour are in scope, under this milestone.

1. Write the rule for each item in the [collisions page](../../simulation/collisions.md) before any code: the input (contact, speed, what was hit), the output (passenger state, emergency response), and what is measured. Mark the page's "Not yet" list as decided for each item.
2. Passengers: after a collision, passengers may want to leave the vehicle. Specify the trigger and the door behaviour, built on the passenger model of [M-0101](../phase-4/M-0101.md) and the load model of [M-0124](../phase-12/M-0124.md).
3. Police and ambulance: a collision with injury or a blocked road may call police or ambulance. Specify which roaming emergency vehicles respond, using the vehicle groups of [ISS-054](../../../dev/issues/ISS-054-roaming-emergency-services.md) and the map data that names them, not a fixed list.
4. Give the chain push along a trailer its own test if it is in scope, as [ISS-044](../../../dev/issues/ISS-044-collision-response-beyond-first-version.md) item 3 asks. The trailer seam test of ISS-011 is not part of this milestone.

**Out of scope:** copying OMSI or LOTUS accident scripts or text ([ADR-0002](../../adr/0002-clean-room-policy.md)); a damage model for the vehicle; insurance and money effects (these belong to [M-0156](../phase-16/M-0156.md) if they are wanted).

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. Each item of the collisions page's "Not yet" list is marked either in scope with a task in this milestone, or out of scope with a reason.
2. The passenger and emergency rules are written in prose on the collisions page before any code, with the input, output and measurement for each.
3. If the chain push is in scope, a test shows that the push spreads along the chain and fails when the spread is removed.
4. Any code change is in its own task of this milestone and names M-0171 in its commit.
