## 🎯 Objective

Every OMSI format module (`formats/vehicles/`, `formats/scenery/`, `formats/o3d/`, the map, timetable, text and the other legacy readers) lives in `opts-omsi`, with its tests and corpus tests, and no OMSI-specific logic stays in the shared crates.

**Rules fixed by the task (all milestones of the phase):** no circular dependency (shared domain types sit in a lower crate that both parsers use); `opts-sim`, `opts-bevy` and `opts-game` must not depend on `opts-omsi`; `opts-omsi` is a leaf adapter used by the importer; parser behaviour does not change (tests pass identically before and after); no OMSI parsing logic is deleted; files move with `git mv` so `git log --follow` works; no new workspace dependency without approval; nothing reaches `master` without the project lead's approval; checkpoints after phases A and B.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [x] B3 `git mv` the format modules into `opts-omsi`, update imports, remove OMSI-specific logic from the shared crate; `cargo check -p opts-omsi` passes.
- [x] The corpus tests (`hof_corpus`, `timetable_corpus`, `world_parse` and the others) keep running and give identical numbers; the real-installation tests stay opt-in as today.
- [x] `opts-core` keeps a facade re-exporting the moved modules while the other crates migrate (the ADR sets the date it goes).
- [x] Parser behaviour is unchanged: the golden and corpus outputs before and after are compared and attached to the report.

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- The run-time loading path of `opts-game` and `opts-bevy` reads OMSI files today (see the open points of [M-0131](M-0131.md)): moving the parsers must not leave those crates depending on `opts-omsi`; the adapter or the dated exception of the ADR carries them.
- Churn: 55 + 49 + 20 files change imports; do it in batches, each green.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. All tests, including the corpus tests, pass with identical numbers.
2. `opts-omsi` depends on no engine crate; `opts-sim` and `opts-bevy` do not depend on `opts-omsi` (checked by `crate_graph.py`).
