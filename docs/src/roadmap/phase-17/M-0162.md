## 🎯 Objective

Draw power from an overhead wire for trolleybuses and trams, through a pantograph state and an overhead-line input.

## 🧩 Scope

1. Research note: the pantograph and wire variables used by the corpus scripts (counts and prose only), and the trolleybus vehicles present.
2. Design page, reviewed before code.
3. First slice: pantograph state and a power input that is zero with no contact.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. The design page is approved.
2. A test: no contact gives zero power; contact gives power.

## ⚠️ Risks

- Large; affects the consist power model. Start only after the research note is accepted.
