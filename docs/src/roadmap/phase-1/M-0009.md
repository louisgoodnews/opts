## 🎯 Objective
Document the repaint mechanism end to end: `[CTC]`/`[CTCTexture]` hooks, `.cti` files, repaint names, slots, `[setvar]`, assignment through ailists and vehicle selection, templates.

> ℹ️ **Scope origin.** Proposed in [`formats/gap-analysis.md`](../../formats/gap-analysis.md) and approved by the project lead on 2026-10-04 ("Proceed"). The ROADMAP gained the item through P-002 step 4.

## 🔍 Scope
**In Scope:**
- `.cti`, `[CTC]`, `[CTCTexture]`, `Vehicles/*/templates`, `.off` variants.
- Human colour schemes and scenery repaints where they use the same mechanism.

**Out of Scope:**
- The RepaintTool and its `.rpc` internals beyond the file list.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [x] `formats/text/repaints.md`: chain, folder conventions, slot naming, `[setvar]` use, assignment, templates, statistics.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria (Definition of Done)
1. The chain from `[CTC]` to the texture file is documented with at least three traced real examples (paraphrased).
2. Slot names and their frequency are tabulated.
3. `model_cfg` Q-011 (`[CTC]` parameters) is answered or narrowed.
4. Where the chosen repaint is stored/selected is stated or listed as open.
5. Each spec follows the OPTS spec template (metadata, overview, structure, field reference, examples, Open Questions, Provenance, related documents), is linked from `SUMMARY.md` and the formats overview, and its Provenance states "No OMSI binaries were inspected".
6. `formats/gap-analysis.md` is updated to show the gap as closed (or partly closed).

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Selection behaviour needs OMSI tests. | Mark ❓ and add to the mutation kit proposals. |
| Shipped text is copyrighted. | Paraphrase; list only facts (names, counts, parameter lists); cite the file path. |
| Meaning inferred from names may be wrong. | Mark ❓; add Open Questions; propose a mutation test where behaviour matters. |
