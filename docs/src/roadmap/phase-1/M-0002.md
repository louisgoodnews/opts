## 🎯 Objective
Document OMSI's mesh and texture formats and the coordinate conventions, because every rendering feature depends on them.

## 🔍 Scope
**In Scope:**
- `.o3d` and chunk reference, `.x`, texture formats.
- Coordinate conversion rules (`architecture/coordinates.md`).

**Out of Scope:**
- Rust parsers and Bevy loaders (listed on the BOARD under this milestone but belonging to Phase 2/3; they stay blocked and should be re-homed when those phases get milestones).

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria (Definition of Done)
1. Each spec follows the OPTS spec template (metadata table, overview, structure, field reference, examples, Open Questions, Provenance, related documents).
2. Every claim cites its evidence in the spec's Provenance; unknowns are listed as Open Questions.
3. No OMSI binary was inspected and no asset is included.
4. The spec is marked 🟡 Draft until the maintainer review and the relevant mutation tests are done; 🟢 Specified requires the spec's high-priority Open Questions to be resolved.
5. `architecture/coordinates.md` exists and states handedness, axes and units with evidence.
