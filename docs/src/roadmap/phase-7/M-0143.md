## 🎯 Objective

A player without a keyboard on the driving hand can drive: the cursor is the pedals and the wheel.

## 📋 Behaviour

| Input | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| Press the mouse wheel (a click: no drag, under 0.4 s) | Switches the mode on or off. A wheel *drag* still pans the external camera. |
| Cursor height, mode on | The middle line of the window is the threshold: above it throttle (0 at the middle, 1 at the top edge), below it the service brake (0 at the middle, 1 at the bottom edge). A dead zone of 4 % around the middle line. |
| Cursor width, mode on | Steering: straight at the middle, full lock at the left and right edges (same dead zone). |
| Right *click* (no drag, under 0.4 s), mode on | Frees the mouse: the pedals and the wheel are let go, cockpit clicks and the menus work with the cursor, the keyboard drives as usual. The next right click gives the mouse back to the driving. A right *drag* still turns the camera. |
| Cockpit clicks (left button on a hot-spot) | Ignored while the mouse drives (the cursor is the wheel); they work again when the mouse is freed. |
| The vehicle takes no input (free camera, a dialog) | The mouse does not drive; what it held is released once. |

The mouse overrides the keyboard's pedals and wheel while it drives. The throttle works in the gear the vehicle is in (Drive; the gear keys are unchanged). A cue shows the mode: thin lines at the middle of the window and a line of text with the way out (the text says "Mouse free" and the way back when freed).

`controls.mouse_driving` (Controls page, default on) switches the feature off: the wheel press then does nothing. `drive.state` reports `mouse_driving {enabled, on, freed, steering}`.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. With the mode on, the cursor at the top edge gives full throttle, at the bottom edge full brake, at the left and right edges full lock, at the middle nothing.
2. A wheel click switches the mode, a right click frees and takes back the mouse; a right drag turns the camera without freeing it.
3. The setting switches the mode off; `./scripts/local-ci.sh` passes.

## 📝 Notes & Logs

*First slice 2026-10-20.* Verified live on the Citybus 530 through `input.mouse.click` (middle, right) and `input.mouse.position`: mode on at the middle gives nothing; the top edge gives throttle 1.00, a quarter up 0.48, the top-left corner throttle 0.99 and steer -0.99, the bottom-right corner brake 0.99 and steer 0.99; a right click frees (pedals 0, `steering` false, the cursor in a corner changes nothing) and the next gives the mouse back; the wheel press ends the mode and lets the pedals go. The cue (two thin lines at the middle and the line of text) is checked on a screenshot.
