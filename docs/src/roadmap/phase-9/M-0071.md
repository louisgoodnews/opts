## 🎯 Objective

Drive information is bundled in **one cluster in the bottom-left corner** (control hints, speed, gear, parking brake, alerts) and a **mini map sits in the bottom-right corner**, within the existing UI standards (tokens, registry, no focus, fixed slots, contrast).

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [x] Update the slot model in [HUD registry](../../architecture/hud-registry.md): `ClusterBottomLeft` (instruments, hints, alerts in a fixed vertical order) and `MiniMap` (bottom-right) replace the bottom-centre instruments; status bar and camera label unchanged; profiles (RON) keep working (migrate the shipped four).
- [x] **Mini map widget:** top-down render of the loaded tiles' roads/terrain around the player (render-to-texture or a vector draw of lane/spline geometry), player and session-vehicle markers with heading, zoom in/out, north-up or heading-up (setting), scales 100 m–1 km; cheap (budget ≤ 1 ms, updated at ≤ 10 Hz); toggle in *Settings → Interface* and a key.
- [x] Contrast and legibility on any backdrop (panel with minimum contrast as in design-language §9); colour-blind safe markers.
- [x] Layout tests across window sizes and UI scales (the a11y matrix, [M-0065](M-0065.md)), no overlap with toasts or the camera label.
- [x] Docs: update design-language, interaction-and-feel and screens pages; golden screenshot of the HUD (fixture content only).

## 🧾 As built (2026-10-13)

Slots `ClusterBottomLeft` (rows hints, alerts, instruments in a fixed order) and `MiniMap`; the four shipped RON profiles are migrated and old slot names still load. The mini map draws the **road paths of the lane graph** (no rails, no terrain; the terrain code is untouched) into one texture at most 10 times per second; zoom 100/200/500/1000 m (`+`/`-`), north-up or heading-up (Shift+F7), F7 switches it off; settings `hud.minimap`, `hud.minimap_zoom`, `hud.minimap_orientation`; control method `hud.minimap` and `minimap` in `hud.state`. Toasts stack above the map (`ToastInset`); the map hides while the viewpoint editor is open (the editor panel moved to 68 px from the top). Tests: `hud::minimap::tests`, `hud::tests`, `tests/hud_matrix.rs` (1024x640 to 2560x1440, UI scale 75 to 200 %), golden `hud-1280x720-{bright,dark}`. Live on Berlin-Spandau: markers and heading agree with `vehicle.get` (306.6 deg), F7 and `+` work, a redraw takes about 0.7 ms at 200 m (the 1 km view and a busy machine showed 1 to 5 ms in wall-clock time, which includes being descheduled). Not done: rails and terrain on the map, route line, panning.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- The HUD shows the cluster bottom-left and the mini map bottom-right at 1024×640 up to 2560×1440 and UI scale 75–200 % without overlap; the mini map follows the vehicle and can be switched off; the layout passes the a11y rule matrix.
