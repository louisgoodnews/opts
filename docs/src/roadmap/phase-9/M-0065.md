## 🎯 Objective

Make the interface usable by more people and prove it with people.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [ ] **WCAG 2.2 AA audit** of all screens and themes (checklist and findings table); fixes; High-contrast theme verified; colour-blind simulation in the contrast test.
- [ ] Reduced motion and flashing limits verified; UI scale 75–200 % verified at three window sizes without clipping.
- [ ] **Gamepad and wheel navigation** of every menu on the action layer; documented device profiles.
- [ ] Screen-reader feasibility: result of the M-0061 spike turned into a decision (implement through Bevy's accessibility support, or document the limitation and the alternative).
- [ ] **German and English complete**, pseudo-locale and long-string tests passing; language selectable at first run and in Settings; documented process for community translations.
- [ ] **Usability test**: a moderated protocol (5 participants, 6 tasks: first start, plan and start a drive, change a setting, find controls, report a problem, leave a drive; measures: success, time, errors, perceived effort, HUD glance times) with a results report in `docs/src/meta/` and a list of resulting fixes.
- [ ] Golden screenshots, UI performance budget and CI rules green across the whole application.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- AA findings table has no open 🔴; all keyboard and gamepad traversals pass.
- ≥ 4 of 5 participants complete every task without help; failures are logged as findings.
- The report's rules that the tests could not cover are listed as open items with owners.
