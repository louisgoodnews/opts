## 🎯 Objective

In *Plan a drive* the player can pick a timetable run to drive. The step appears only when the plan has runs to offer; choosing one starts the bus at the run's first stop instead of the start point, and the drive follows the run (IBIS, destination boards, `GetTT*`, the route on the maps).

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [x] **Which runs.** `trips::list_trips`: the runs of the map's `TTData` whose line file is marked `[userallowed]`, that run on the plan's weekday (the line file's days, [`line_runs_on`](../../architecture/scheduled-traffic.md), and the short days in the tour's name: `Mo-Fr 6`, `Sa 4`, `So 2`, `Sa+So`, `trips::tour_runs_on`), that depart within an hour after the plan's clock (a run after midnight counts for a late clock) and whose first stop is a bus stop of `Busstops.cfg`; earliest first. Only for a road vehicle driven alone (no train, no drive without a vehicle). The timetables are read once per map off the main thread (`TripState`).
- [x] **The step.** `PlannerStep::Trip` between *When* and *Review*, shown only while runs are offered (`PlannerState::trips_offered`; *Next*, *Back*, `[`/`]`, `Esc` and *Enter* skip it otherwise; the stepper hides its button and numbers the steps shown). A master-detail list without stars: *No trip* first (start at the start point), then `08:45  92 → Stadtgrenze` with the first stop and the tour; the detail names departure, first stop, number of stops, last stop and tour. A chosen trip that no longer fits (other map, vehicle, date or time) is dropped; leaving the conditions on the step moves to *Review*.
- [x] **Where the drive starts.** The trip replaces the start point: the summary line and the review show the first stop (`Freudstr (08:45 92 → Stadtgrenze)`), the review has a *Trip* row. The load finds the stop object whose ID code is the station id (placed in its tile or attached to a spline, the same lookup as the entry points) and puts the bus there with its front at the stop: the `.bus` bounding box gives the distance from the vehicle's origin to its front (5.7 m for the articulated MAN NG272, whose origin is in the front unit; 6 m without a bounding box; `find_stop_spawn`, since 2026-10-26); without such an object it starts at the start point with a warning.
- [x] **Following the run.** Once the player's vehicle has its route and scripts, the run is selected as `bus.set_timetable` does (`trip_start::follow_pending_trip`): IBIS keys, destination, `GetTT*`, the HUD line `Line 5: next Nervenklinik 8:47`, the route on the maps.
- [x] **A vacant stop.** The scheduled traffic leaves the player's run alone (`ScheduledTraffic::player_run`, set whenever the player's vehicle follows a run, also from the vehicle menu); scheduled and unscheduled vehicles appear at least 40 m from the camera, so at the start no other vehicle stands at the player's stop.
- [x] **Protocol.** `scene.load_map {trip: "LINEFILE:TOUR:RUN"}` (1.28): the same start for scripts; the stop and texts are read from the map's timetables during the load; an unknown run is dropped with a warning.
- [x] **Fixed on the way.** `validate` compared the plan's vehicle with display names only; a vehicle whose name another one shares is kept by its id, so the *Review* step said "Choose a vehicle first" and *Drive* stayed disabled (the MAN NG272 `man-gn92-main` next to `man-gn92-main-eur`).
- [x] Tests: runs offered by window, weekday and `[userallowed]`, a run after midnight, the short day names of tours, the step skipped without runs, `parse_trip`.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Berlin-Spandau, MAN NG272, Saturday 31 December 1994 08:45: the step *Trip* shows 34 runs of lines 5, 5E and 92 (Saturday tours only); choosing *08:47 5 → U Ruhleben* and *Drive* starts the bus at Nervenklinik in the bus bay, the board shows line 5, the HUD the next stop and the time to it (checked 2026-10-26 through `ui.*`).
- [x] `scene.load_map {trip: "92:6:10"}` on Friday 30 December 1994 12:00 starts the articulated bus at Freudstr. with its front at the stop sign, the board shows `92 HEERSTR. / STADTGRENZE`, the mini map the route.
- [x] Without runs (another time, a tram, *Drive without a vehicle*) the wizard has its four steps as before.

## ❓ Open

- Runs already under way at the chosen clock are not offered (only departures within the hour after it); starting in the middle of a run is not planned.
- Spawning at a stop during a running session (the vehicle menu's places) does not exist yet; at the start the stop is vacant by construction.
