## 🎯 Objective

Bring the workspace back under the standards in `docs/src/contributing/standards.md`: files ≤ 500 lines, functions ≤ 80 lines, no panicking calls in library code, no stubs, warning-free rustdoc.

- Fix the six rustdoc warnings; enforce `cargo doc -D warnings` in CI (with M-0042).

**Out of Scope:**
