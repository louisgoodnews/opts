## 🎯 Objective

Distance-based level-of-detail switching, shadow-distance culling and mesh instancing. The user-facing **view distance** setting of the settings page (`graphics.view_distance`, [settings registry](../../architecture/settings-registry.md)) is registered as "Feature not available yet!" until this milestone delivers a distance the user can change.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [~] Dynamic LOD mesh switching and shadow culling in `opts-bevy`. *(status sync 2026-10-25: scenery objects switch their own `[LOD]` bands in the scenery runtime (2026-10-12, [scenery runtime](../../architecture/scenery-runtime.md)); `graphics.detail_distance` biases the band distance, `graphics.shadow_distance` sets the sun cascades' range (default 300 m), `graphics.light_distance` the map-light pool radius, all live (`4d2a4955`, 2026-10-24, `settings_registry::live`). Open: LOD of tile/terrain meshes, per-object shadow culling.)* *(2026-11-05, `agent/m0029-lod`: per-part shadow culling built (`graphics.shadow_detail`, `scenery_shadow_cull_system`) and measured with `scripts/shadow-cull-bench.sh` on Berlin-Spandau: 44 % fewer shadow casters, no change in frame time or shadow-pass GPU time, so it stays off and hidden ([lod-culling.md](../../architecture/lod-culling.md) §3.1). Open: terrain LOD, decided by a measurement first.)*
- [x] A view-distance setting (`graphics.view_distance`, 1 to 8): scenery objects whose bounding sphere lies beyond `150 m × step` from the main camera are hidden (default 4 = 600 m, the top step draws everything loaded), with 20 m hysteresis, checked every 0.25 s, live. The *not available yet* place `settings.view-distance` is removed. Tiles follow `gameplay.tile_radius`; shadows and instancing are not touched.
- [~] Shadow-distance culling and mesh instancing ([M-0082](M-0082.md) research). *(status sync 2026-10-25: the shadow distance is a setting now (see above); the instancing research is done and recommends asset de-duplication and optional static merging (prototype `OPTS_INSTANCING`, off by default); no productised instancing yet.)*

- [x] *(2026-11-05)* Design page [lod-culling.md](../../architecture/lod-culling.md): what decides detail, culling and shadows today, the measured costs (Berlin-Spandau is CPU-bound at 3.7–3.9 ms with 0.17–0.25 ms of shadow-pass GPU time), and the order of work: per-part shadow culling gated on frame time, asset de-duplication, then measurements that decide terrain LOD and occlusion culling.
