## 🎯 Objective

Research how to implement **reflections in mirrors and glass surfaces** and, later, **wet surfaces** (rain: roads and vehicles), which approach fits OPTS's constraints (frame time, many materials, OMSI content with mirror and glass materials), and produce a prototype and recommendation.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [ ] **Content facts first:** how mirrors and glass are defined in the OMSI model/material data (mirror materials, `[mirror]`-type keys or dedicated meshes, alpha-blended glass, environment-map keys, `[matl_envmap]`-style entries; check docs/src/formats); corpus statistics: how many vehicles and objects use them; what the original does (planar mirror textures rendered per frame, cubemaps).
- [ ] **Approach survey** with Bevy 0.20: screen-space reflections (SSR), light probes / environment map and reflection probes, planar reflections with a mirrored camera and render-to-texture (for rear-view mirrors), cube-map capture, ray-tracing options (hardware requirements), specular occlusion, transmission/clearcoat in `StandardMaterial` for glass; cost per technique; interplay with MSAA, tonemapping and the Phase 8 looks; DLSS.
- [ ] **Prototype (branch `research/reflections`):** (a) planar mirror render for a vehicle's rear-view mirrors at reduced resolution and rate; (b) glass with environment reflections and transmission on a bus windscreen and a building window; (c) a wet-surface material parameter set (roughness drop, darkening, puddle mask) on road materials driven by a `Wetness` resource, with SSR or probe-based reflections; each switchable and measured.
- [ ] **Measurements:** frame time and memory per technique on three scenes (cockpit with mirrors, city street, rain), visual evidence outside the repository; quality versus cost table.
- [ ] **Report** `docs/src/architecture/reflections-research.md`: findings with numbers, recommendation, phased plan (mirrors first, glass next, wet surfaces with the weather milestone), settings (quality levels, reflections off), and proposed implementation milestones.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- The report has measured numbers for each prototyped technique, a clear recommendation and a milestone proposal; the branch builds and passes the gates; default rendering is unchanged unless a switch is on.
