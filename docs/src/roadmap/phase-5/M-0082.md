## 🎯 Objective

Find out how much and how safely OPTS can leverage Bevy's **instancing** for meshes that are used repeatedly (lanterns, trees, houses, signs, parked cars, wheels, bus parts), and decide a design. Research first: measurements, a prototype and a recommendation, on a research branch.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [ ] **Inventory:** statistics over the corpus and loaded scenes of how often the same mesh+material pair repeats per tile and per map (objects per unique `.o3d`/`.sco` and per unique material/texture), draw-call and entity counts of representative scenes, current frame-time split (CPU render-prep versus GPU).
- [ ] **Bevy mechanisms:** document how Bevy 0.20 batches and instances (automatic batching/instancing of entities sharing mesh and material handles, GPU preprocessing and indirect drawing, `NoAutomaticBatching`, custom instancing via a custom material/pipeline with an instance buffer, mesh/material asset sharing); what breaks batching in OPTS today (per-object materials, per-object colour/alpha/light-map variants, per-mesh script-driven animation and visibility, unique handles from the decode cache, per-tile asset duplication); source links to the Bevy documentation and release notes.
- [ ] **Prototype on the branch** `research/mesh-instancing`: (a) asset de-duplication so equal meshes and materials share handles across objects and tiles (keyed by content hash); (b) verify Bevy's automatic batching kicks in (draw call counts via the diagnostics / `render.stats`); (c) a manual instancing path for static scenery (instance buffer with transform and per-instance tint/light-map index) for the most repeated objects; (d) handling of animated or script-driven objects (excluded or per-instance animation data).
- [ ] **Measurements** before/after on at least three maps (dense city, rural, depot): draw calls, entities, frame time at fixed camera paths, memory, load time; with `scripts/visual-ab.sh`-style image comparison to prove that nothing changes visually (image metrics tool of Phase 8).
- [ ] **Risks and compatibility:** per-object variation (repaints, light maps `.LM`, seasonal switches, alpha scale), culling and LOD interplay (see tile eviction), Windows/macOS/GPU driver differences, DLSS.
- [ ] **Report** `docs/src/architecture/instancing-research.md`: findings with numbers, options compared, recommendation, estimated effort, implementation milestone proposal (to be added to the roadmap).

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- The report contains measured numbers on three maps, a clear recommendation (adopt / adopt partly / reject) and a milestone proposal; the prototype branch builds and passes the gates; visual comparison shows no unintended changes.
