## 🎯 Objective

The driver can sell a ticket for cash, take the fare, and give change through the keys the manual names. The money model and the change action are new; `GiveChangeCoin` is currently logged only.

## 🧩 Scope

1. Money model per trip and per passenger (fare from the ticket pack's prices, M-0101).
2. Cash desk interaction and change action in the cab, with the key bindings listed in Help.
3. The `GivenTicket` and `GiveChangeCoin` script callbacks act on the model.
4. Ticket printer interaction confirmed on the printer the vehicle has.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. A test: a cash sale gives the expected change for a given coin set.
2. The keys are listed in the Help table (reverse test of [M-0164](../phase-17/M-0164.md)).
3. Dated in-game check: one sale and its change on a bus with the printer.

## ⚠️ Risks

- Scope creep into a full economy; keep it to fare, change and the drawer.
- Fare computation from the stop (RG-12, [ISS-053](../../../dev/issues/ISS-053-stop-dependent-fares.md)) is not in this scope. The feature research proposes adding it; the owner has not decided. Until then the money model takes the ticket pack's prices only.
