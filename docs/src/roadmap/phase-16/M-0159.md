## 🎯 Objective

Play a greeting when a passenger boards, using the voice of the ticket pack the map uses, through the audio system.

## 🧩 Scope

1. Audio payload on the boarding event (deferred item P6).
2. Voice choice per ticket pack; one clip per greeting.
3. Playback through the audio system, with the Audio settings for volume.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. A test chooses the clip for a given pack and boarding event.
2. Dated run: a boarding plays one greeting at the expected distance.

## ⚠️ Risks

- Many clips per voice; keep the first slice to one greeting per pack.
