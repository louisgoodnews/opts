## 🎯 Objective

Wash the bus at a depot and tank it at a depot or station, taking time as OMSI does.

## 🧩 Scope

1. Bind the `veh_wash` trigger (unbound in the corpus scan, M-0120 notes) to a wash action in a depot zone.
2. Tanking at a depot or station takes a modelled time; F12 stays as the quick key.
3. Dirt state from the wash feeds M-0120.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. A wash in a depot zone changes the dirt state; a test covers it.
2. Tanking at a station takes the modelled time; a test covers it.
3. Dated in-game check at a depot.

## ⚠️ Risks

- The wash trigger's meaning is confirmed from the scripts only (prose), not assumed.
