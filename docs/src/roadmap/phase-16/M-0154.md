## 🎯 Objective

A passenger alights at the stop of their destination. Today the ride length decides, and the bus's `set_stop` coupling does not drive it.

## 🧩 Scope

1. Couple the alighting decision to the stop the bus has reached (`bus.set_stop`) and the timetable stop sequence.
2. Test with three stops: a passenger for stop 2 alights at stop 2 and not before.
3. Update passengers.md §4 with the rule.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. The three-stop test passes, and fails when the coupling is removed.
2. Dated in-game check on one timetabled line.

## ⚠️ Risks

- Depends on the timetable stop list being complete for the map; fall back to the ride length when a run has no stop list, and say so in the docs.
