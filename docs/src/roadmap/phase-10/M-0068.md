## 🎯 Objective

A session is a map plus **zero or more user vehicles**. The player can start with the map only, spawn buses during the session, remember all of them, switch the active one and **despawn** any of them (the existing respawn of the active bus stays; correction of the report 2026-10-13: the wish was *despawn*, not *respawn*).

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [x] **Map only:** the *What* step of the wizard offers *Drive without a vehicle* (and the vehicle selection screen of the legacy menu a matching button); the session starts with a free camera, the simulation running, no HUD instruments; a vehicle can be spawned later.
- [x] **Session vehicle registry** (`SessionVehicles` resource): every user-spawned vehicle gets a stable session id, its catalogue reference, repaint, spawn point and live state (alive, destroyed, despawned by streaming). Parked/AI vehicles are not part of it.
- [x] **Switch active vehicle:** list of session vehicles in *Pause → Vehicle* and a quick-switch key; switching moves the cockpit camera, HUD profile and input to the chosen one; the previous keeps its state (parking brake on, brought to rest by the physics, or stays under AI later).
- [x] **Despawn any:** *Despawn* removes a chosen session vehicle (with its trailer chain), also an inactive one and the active one (then the next session vehicle becomes active, or none); with confirmation; the *Respawn* of the active vehicle (reset to its spawn point) stays as it is today.
- [x] **Spawn-place name (R-12):** every session vehicle records the *name of the map entry point it was spawned at*; the HUD place label shows it for the active vehicle ([M-0075](../phase-9/M-0075.md)).
- [x] Persistence hook: the registry is a section of the session save ([M-0069](M-0069.md)).
- [x] Control protocol: `session.vehicles`, `session.spawn`, `session.switch`, `session.despawn` plus tests.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- A session can start without any vehicle, spawn two buses, switch between them and despawn the inactive one; all three verified through the control protocol and a headless test.
- No state leaks: despawning the session removes every session vehicle and its trailer chain; streaming a tile out never removes a user vehicle silently (a warning toast appears instead).

## 📝 Implementation notes (2026-10-13)

* Decision (R-03): without a vehicle the camera is the free camera over the chosen start point, the HUD shows its status bar, hints and pause button but **no instruments**, and the simulation runs. *Drive without a vehicle* is a pinned entry of the wizard's vehicle list and a button in the vehicle detail (`wizard.what.none`); it is remembered in the drive history.
* Switching parks the previous vehicle (parking brake on) and gives the new one `PlayerVehicle`; **F8** / Shift+F8 cycle (Help table row), *Pause → Vehicle* lists the vehicles with *Switch*, *Respawn* and *Despawn* (the last behind the shell's confirmation dialog).
* Despawn removes the vehicle, its trailer chain and every child; the most recently spawned other vehicle becomes active, or none (free camera).
* The registry serialises as the save section `session_vehicles` (version 1) for [M-0069](M-0069.md).
* Verified live (real game, Berlin-Spandau, debug build, throwaway XDG): map-only start; two buses spawned through `session.spawn` at two different entry points; `session.switch`; driving 85 m with the place label vanishing after about 25 m; despawn of the inactive and of the active vehicle; *Pause → Vehicle* rows, F8 and the despawn dialog (cancel and confirm).
* Not done: vehicles do not collide with each other; the vehicle's current pose and state are not part of the save section yet (the save system adds them); streaming never removes vehicles, so the *warning toast on streaming* fires only for an entity that vanished for another reason.
