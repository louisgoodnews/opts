## 🎯 Objective

Maps show water: rivers, lakes and the sea defined by the `.water` file next to each tile are loaded, rendered with a believable surface and, later, handled by physics (buoyancy is out of scope; vehicles must not drive on water silently).

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [x] **Format first** ([ADR-0002](../../adr/0002-clean-room-policy.md), docs first): resolve the open semantics of the four level values in the tile spec (corner versus quadrant versus per-region level) by corpus analysis and mutation test; update `formats/map/tiles.md`. *(done 2026-10-16: corner levels shared with the neighbours; marker semantics and in-tile interpolation remain ❓ Q-M07)*
- [x] Parse `.water` in `opts-core` (structure, units, tests over the 1,485 corpus files). *(`MapWater::level_at`, corpus test `water_corpus`)*
- [x] Build the water surface mesh per tile in `opts-bevy` where the level is above the terrain; handle tile borders without seams. *(`opts_bevy::build_water_mesh`; seams across tiles not yet checked by screenshot)*
- [x] Water material: Phase 8 compatible (reality-calibrated colour, transparency, depth tint); reflection/foam are stretch goals, not acceptance. *(first version: translucent blue-green, no depth tint)*
- [ ] Water texture/material files that the map references (check the tile and `global.cfg` records) are honoured.
- [~] Interaction rules: AI/vehicle behaviour on water documented (blocked or allowed with a warning); a setting to disable water. *(setting `graphics.water` done; vehicle behaviour on water not yet)*
- [ ] Control protocol and benchmark: screenshot recipe for a water tile.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- At least five corpus maps with water show water at the right level and extent in a screenshot comparison; no seams at tile borders.
- The `.water` spec page no longer lists open questions for the parsed fields; tests parse all corpus files without error.
