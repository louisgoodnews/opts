## 🎯 Objective

Maps show water: rivers, lakes and the sea defined by the `.water` file next to each tile are loaded, rendered with a believable surface and, later, handled by physics (buoyancy is out of scope; vehicles must not drive on water silently).

### Screenshot recipe (windowed run; headless runs do not update `GlobalTransform`, so the camera does not stream tiles there)

1. Pick a tile with water: marker `[water]` in the `.map`, a `.water` file, and terrain below the level (`corner levels` > `.terrain` heights). Examples: `Palma` tile 480/7852 (sea, level −5), `Lucerne_Line_24_` 1518/9727 (lake, level 437), `Lemmental_Neuenbreid` 7/−5 (river, level −0.5), `HafenCityHamburg` −11/−3.
2. `opts --windowed --map-path <map> --cli-port N`, then `opts-cli --port N camera.set_mode --mode free` and `camera.teleport <x> <y> <level + 60..120> --yaw 0 --pitch -35` with `x = (tile + 1) · tile_size` (the log line `tile size` gives it; `tile_size` is 300 m on ordinary maps, 416.6 m on Lucerne, 471.6 m on Palma) to look at the border to the east neighbour.
3. Wait about 10 s for the tiles to stream, then `opts-cli --port N screenshot --name water`.
4. `opts-cli --port N settings.set --key graphics.water --value false` (and `settings.reset` afterwards: the value is stored) shows the ground below for comparison.
5. `opts-cli --port N vehicle.teleport --x <x> --y <y>` puts the player's vehicle into the water; the WARN `the player's vehicle is under water` appears in the log.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- At least five corpus maps with water show water at the right level and extent in a screenshot comparison; no seams at tile borders.
- The `.water` spec page no longer lists open questions for the parsed fields; tests parse all corpus files without error.
