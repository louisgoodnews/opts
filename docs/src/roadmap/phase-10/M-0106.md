## 🎯 Objective

A player composes a train of rail cars in the spawn menu, spawns it on a track and drives it from the first car; formations can be saved, reloaded and started from the installation's `.zug` presets.

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** `.zug` parser and `TrainFormation`; validation; the consist spawner and `ConsistLoad` dynamics; the Formation tab of *Select Vehicle*; TOML store; protocol commands; session-save field; `docs/src/formats/text/zug.md`.

**Out of scope:** AI trains from `.zug` (M-0107, proposed), cab switching and scripts of passive cars (M-0098), rigid-body couplers, `.zug` writing, 3D preview, track picker, group-name resolution, signals and timetables.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

1. [ ] **C1** `opts_core::formats::trains` (model, parser, notes), spec page, fixtures, corpus test.
2. [ ] **C2** resolver and spawner, `ConsistLoad`, `behind_of_next` extraction, `formation.validate` and `formation.spawn`.
3. [ ] **C3** Formation tab (add, remove, reorder, turn, summary, messages).
4. [ ] **C4** TOML store, `formation.save/load/list`, `SessionVehicleRecord.formation`.
5. [ ] **C5** verification: single NF6D, a three-car formation with the middle car turned, save/restart/load, empty and one-car formations rejected, a `.zug` preset spawned.
6. [ ] Final report `docs/src/meta/train-formation-implementation-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. All 204 regular `.zug` files of the installation parse; the 18 group-name files parse as groups; `RUHR.zug` is rejected; no OMSI file is committed.
2. Acceleration of a formation scales with `1 / total mass` within 2 % at equal traction; braking uses the braked mass; the old single-head behaviour is covered by a test that states the change.
3. A three-car formation spawns on a track, each car in the right place in a curve, with the middle car turned; the first car is drivable.
4. Every validation rule V1-V8 has a test and a message in German and English; an invalid formation cannot be spawned.
5. A saved formation survives restart; a session save contains the formation and restores it.
6. `./scripts/local-ci.sh` passes; no per-vehicle special case; `articulation.rs` unchanged.

## ⚠️ Risks

Q-TF-01 (powered cars), Q-TF-02 (usable end of a part chain), Q-TF-03 (flip of chain units), Q-TF-04 (part limit), Q-TF-06 (`.ovh` head): see the design, section 12. The NF6D-style part chains are a stretch goal; the milestone is complete with self-contained cars.
