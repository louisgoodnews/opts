## 🎯 Objective

**Deliverable: the BRT C2 mesh loads and displays completely and accurately in the simulation.** Every part the vehicle's data declares is built, bound to the right material and texture, placed and animated as authored, and drawn; whatever is not drawn is a documented content defect with evidence, not an unexplained gap.

The vehicle: `Vehicles/BRT_C2-Solo/C2_2T.bus` (the solo bus; its AI variants and the articulated sibling in the same family are checked with it where they share parts).

**What is known** (do not repeat the work; build on it):

- R-09 was looked at twice without a defect at the distances used: 27 views of the solo bus and its AI variant on Grundorf, 2026-10-19 (MI-0038); all 1,195 meshes of the C2 family and three other Citaro families are plain (not scrambled), without parse, index or extent findings.
- Two findings stand: the textures `t_c2_frontlampe.tga` and `t_c2_3t_nebel.tga` that `Frontleuchten*.o3d` name are not in the add-on (the lamps draw grey); 7 missing textures in the family (MI-0016); 122 small cockpit parts (needles, switches, door seals) flagged `UV_CHAOS` by the UV audit use atlas regions (judged correct, not proven part by part).
- The 10 % vehicle sampling cap of the earlier audits means no complete per-part inventory of this bus exists. That inventory is the first deliverable.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** a complete part census of `C2_2T.bus` (every `[mesh]` of the model config, `[CTC]` repaint folders, sub-models, mirrors, passenger cabin, cockpit, doors, wheels, lights); the load result per part (built, rejected, hidden by a rule, drawn); material and texture binding (alpha, glass, emissive, missing textures and their fallbacks); placement (parent transforms, mirrored parts, pivots); animation and visibility rules the model uses (doors, wipers, lights, indicators, instrument needles, display textures); a regression gate; fixes in the loader or bridge where the cause is OPTS, and a content-defect entry (with evidence) where it is the add-on.

**Out of scope:** physics, sound, scripts' behaviour (M-0097, M-0102), other vehicles except where they share a part with the C2, redrawing or replacing add-on assets, committing any OMSI asset.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

### Census
- [ ] A census tool or test (`opts-tools` or an `opts-bevy` example, no assets committed) that lists, for `C2_2T.bus` and one repaint: every declared part with its source file, whether it loaded, material slots with texture names and whether each texture exists, alpha mode, `[visible]`/animation rules, bounding box and parent. Output as a table in `docs/src/quality/` (facts, no screenshots).
- [ ] Scene-trace comparison (`trace.find` on the spawned vehicle, M-0087): the set of drawn parts equals the census minus the parts a rule hides at rest; every difference explained.

### Mesh and material accuracy
- [ ] Per-part look: close views of every body region, interior, cockpit, undercarriage, wheels, mirrors, doors open and closed (the cockpit parts judged `UV_CHAOS` one by one against their atlas regions, not as a group).
- [ ] Missing textures: find out whether OMSI would resolve them elsewhere (search order of texture folders, the `[CTC]` scheme folders, a shared family folder); implement that rule if it exists; otherwise a defined fallback that is visible as such, and a content-defect entry.
- [ ] Glass, lamps and emissive parts: material parameters read from the `.o3d` material records, night behaviour of the lamps (`[light_enh]`, `[matl_nightmap]` where used).

### Animation and rules
- [ ] Doors, wipers, indicators, brake lights and the instrument cluster behave as the model's rules say (variables and animation entries of the C2 listed and checked one by one; unmapped variables registered).
- [ ] Display textures (destination and interior displays) show their default content or are listed as waiting for [M-0100](../phase-4/M-0100.md).

### Gate
- [ ] A regression test over the census that runs when an OMSI installation is present (skipped, with a clear message, without it): zero parts unexplained; the explained exceptions are an explicit list in the test.
- [ ] Screenshot recipe in the mesh issue registry for the C2 (views and camera positions), evidence outside the repository.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. The census table lists every declared part of the solo C2 with its load result; no entry is "unknown".
2. Every part that is built is drawn when no rule hides it, with the material and texture the data names; the scene trace and the census agree.
3. Each missing or substituted texture is either resolved by an OMSI rule that OPTS now implements or registered as a content defect with evidence; no part draws a grey placeholder without such an entry.
4. The body, interior, cockpit, wheels, mirrors, doors and lights are checked in close views by the agent and confirmed by the project lead (the project lead's look is the acceptance of "accurately").
5. The regression test passes with an installation and skips cleanly without one; all gates pass (`./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no OMSI asset is committed.

---

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Accurate" has no reference image (OMSI cannot be run here) | Data-first criteria (census, bindings, rules) plus the project lead's look; every judgement call is recorded as a question in the registry, not decided silently |
| The defect the project lead sees is not in the sampled views | The census is complete (every part), and close views are per part, not per vehicle |
| A fix for the C2 breaks other Citaro or add-on vehicles | Fixes live in the shared loader or bridge with a test; family checks (`X10_BRT_KI`, `EMT_Citaro`, `KVB_Citaro`) before merging |
