## 🎯 Objective

OPTS provides a **world physics system** (gravity, ground contact, friction, air, collisions, coupling); **each vehicle defines its own behaviour** through its data (mass, centre of mass, axles, wheels, springs and dampers, brake system, drivetrain) which the world respects. Today a generic model dominates.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- Two vehicles with different mass and axle layouts brake and ride visibly and measurably differently in the same scenario; a loaded-down heavy articulated bus needs a longer stopping distance than a light one (test).
- Every parameter used is traceable to a vehicle file field or a documented default; the spec page lists unknown semantics as open questions.
