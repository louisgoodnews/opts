## 🎯 Objective

Shadows (and probably overall lighting) should look more realistic. Research and improve shadow quality within the frame budget, aligned with the reality-calibrated Phase 8 look.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [ ] **Assess the current state:** cascaded shadow map settings (cascade count, bounds, resolution, bias/normal bias, filtering), shadow casters selection, light-map interplay, sun direction and ambient, contact/peter-panning artefacts, aliasing at distance; collect reference screenshots and describe what looks wrong.
- [ ] **Options with measurements:** higher resolution and better cascade splits tuned to the view distance; soft shadows (PCF/PCSS options in Bevy 0.20), contact shadows, screen-space ambient occlusion, shadow distance tied to hardware recommendation ([M-0081](../phase-5/M-0081.md)), shadow casting only for relevant objects; quality presets (Off/Low/Medium/High) in the settings registry.
- [ ] Implement the best-value improvements and a *Shadows* setting; check the Vivid/Natural looks and visual-AB metrics; performance numbers per preset.
- [ ] Docs: update the texture-quality/looks docs with the shadow model and presets.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- A documented before/after comparison on three scenes shows visibly more realistic shadows at a measured cost; presets exist and defaults follow the hardware profile.
