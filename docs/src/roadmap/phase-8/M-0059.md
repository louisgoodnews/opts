## 🎯 Objective

Trees, hedges, fences and signs use hard alpha cutouts (`AlphaMode::Mask(0.5)`). With mipmaps they would thin out with distance; without smoothing their edges are stair-stepped. Keep their coverage constant and smooth the edges with the existing 4× MSAA.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- Measured foliage coverage at 150 m is within 3 % of its coverage at 10 m (per reference tree), where it fell by more than 15 % with plain mips.
- Cutout edges show no stair-stepping at 1080p in the reference views (visual check plus the high-frequency metric).
- No new shader or pipeline warnings; all gates pass.
