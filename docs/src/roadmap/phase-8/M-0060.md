## 🎯 Objective

Replace the uncalibrated Bevy defaults with a lighting and colour pipeline anchored in physical reality, and make **Vivid** the default look. OMSI is not a reference.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

### Step 1: Looks and the Vivid default (first, so it can be tested early)
- [ ] A `Look` setting with three presets: **Vivid (default)**, **Natural**, **Bevy default**. A look sets tonemapper, post-saturation, contrast and the ambient scale on the cameras and the sky system; it applies live and is exposed through the control protocol (`render.look`).
- [ ] Provisional *Vivid* values from the proposal's measurements: `SomewhatBoringDisplayTransform`, ambient × 0.5, post-saturation 1.1 (+40 % mean saturation, +20 % contrast on the reference view). They are tuned in step 2 and by the project lead's own testing, which is the acceptance for the default.
- [ ] Settings screen entry; the choice is persisted.

### Step 2: Physical calibration (*Natural*)
- [ ] Specify the model in `docs/src/architecture/` (new page): direct sun at the real clear-sky illuminance for the sun's elevation, sky and ground-bounce ambient derived from it, camera exposure by photographic convention (sunny-16: EV100 ≈ 15 for direct sun, with Bevy's `Exposure::SUNLIGHT`), no hand-picked constants.
- [ ] Reference values with sources: albedo of common surfaces (fresh/aged asphalt, concrete, grass, foliage, snow), the 18 % grey card, sky colour temperature by hour. Every number carries a citation or is marked as an assumption; collected as a table in the spec.
- [ ] `envir.cfg` colours stay as *relative* time-of-day cues (hue shifts at dusk); absolute levels come from the physical model (decision recorded in the spec).
- [ ] Choose the tonemapper by test against a *rendered reference scene* (grey card, colour checker patches, white and black surfaces), not by taste.
- [ ] *Vivid* is defined as *Natural* plus an explicit grade; the difference is documented in numbers.

### Step 3: Verification
- [ ] Reference-surface test: an 18 % grey card in direct midday sun at EV100 15 renders at sRGB 118 ± 10 under *Natural* (automated screenshot test on a synthetic scene).
- [ ] Five times of day × three weather settings × three maps screenshot set; night and dusk checked for regressions (ambient floor, headlights).
- [ ] Metrics reported against the baseline: mean saturation, contrast, darkest pixel.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- The project lead can switch the three looks live and **Vivid is the default** (his testing and report decide step 1).
- *Natural* passes the reference-surface test; the spec lists every constant with its source.
- No night or dusk regression; all gates pass.
