## 🎯 Objective

Trees sway in the wind. **OMSI does not animate trees** (checked 2026-10-26: none of the 7,546 tree-like `.sco` files of the installation has a `[newanim]`; `[tree]` billboards are static crossed quads), so this is an OPTS extension, switchable, that never changes what a file says.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

### Phase A: Specification
- [x] [Vegetation wind](../../architecture/vegetation-wind.md): what OMSI does (nothing), the sway model, wind source (the weather's `[wind]`), setting, performance budget, what is out of scope.

### Phase B: `[tree]` billboards
- [x] A tree material (`StandardMaterial` extended by a vertex stage) that bends the billboard: the base stays, the crown moves with the square of the height; each tree its own phase from its position; a slow sway plus a faster flutter.
- [x] The same bend in the shadow and depth passes (the shadow moves with the crown).
- [x] Wind direction and strength from the weather (`[wind]`), with a gentle breeze as the minimum; updated when the weather changes, not per frame on the CPU.
- [x] Setting `graphics.tree_wind` (on by default), live; off keeps the trees still.
- [x] Tests: the material's wind values follow the weather and the setting; a still tree's vertices are unchanged at strength 0.

### Phase C: Mesh trees
- [x] Trees modelled as `.o3d` meshes (trunk plus foliage cards): an object is a tree when it has no `[tree]` and all its cut-out or blended materials (`[matl_alpha]` 1 or 2) have foliage textures (a word of the name is a foliage word such as `laub`, `branch`, `tanne`, `hecke`; only the generic German words may end a compound). Its non-opaque parts get the tree material with a height-only bend; the opaque trunk stays.
- [x] Tests: foliage names against look-alikes (`SK_Bushokje`, `Lichtentanne_Gelaender`, `Zifferblatt`); a building with a bush texture next to its window glass is no tree; the crown sways and the trunk keeps its material.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- Mesh trees (Bad Hügelsdorf's `Trees_3D`) sway with their crowns; trunks, buildings and fences stand still.
- On Berlin-Spandau and Grundorf, `[tree]` billboards sway visibly in a breeze, more under storm weather, and stand still with the setting off.
- The base of every tree stays fixed; no tree leaves its shadow behind.
- Frame time with the setting on is within 2 % of the setting off on the reference scene.

## 📝 Notes
- Created 2026-10-26 at the project lead's request.
- 2026-10-26, phase B: `opts_bevy::tree_wind` (`TreeMaterial`, `WindTree`, `TreeWindPlugin`), shader `shaders/tree_wind.wesl` (main and prepass vertex stage). A billboard is spawned with its ordinary material and the `WindTree` marker; the plugin swaps in the tree material, so headless runs keep still trees. Checked live on Berlin-Spandau: two screenshots 0.5 s apart differ on the tree crowns, trunks and their shadows; no shader errors. Frame time (same view, 15 s phases): median 9.3–9.7 ms with the wind against 8.9–9.6 ms without, within the run's own noise (phases without any change ranged from 9.6 to 51 ms while tiles stream). About 1,860 tree materials exist on Berlin-Spandau (one per billboard material of the content cache), so a change of the wind or the setting rewrites that many once.
- 2026-10-26, phase C: `opts_game::…::trees::is_mesh_tree` classifies the object at placement (never merged by `OPTS_INSTANCING=merge`); its root gets `MeshTreeRoot`, and `tag_mesh_tree_foliage` gives the non-opaque descendant parts `WindTree` + `MeshTree`. The tree material's second uniform (`shape`) selects the bend: from the texture height (billboards, `1 - v`) or from the height above the object alone (mesh foliage, whose card texture coordinates say nothing about height). A corpus scan found 246 mesh-tree candidates next to 941 billboard `.sco` files. Checked live on Bad Hügelsdorf: 1,162 foliage parts tagged; two screenshots 1.2 s apart differ only on the crowns (trunks, bus, shelters and ground unchanged); no shader errors. The frame time was not measured again: the parts were already separate entities (merging is off by default) and only their material changed.
- 2026-10-26, regression fix (`agent/perf-waldhofen`): the tree material's wind was a plain `#[uniform]`, which made every tree material its own bind group and draw batch (the `StandardMaterial` it extends is bindless and batches across materials). Waldhofen Bahnhof dropped to 2–3 fps: 5,536 alpha-mask and 1,764 shadow draw calls, median 285 ms per frame, against 7 and 105 calls and 41 ms before the tree wind (`a5c68844`, same scene, same machine). The wind is now bindless data (`#[data]` + `#[bindless]`, read through the mesh's material slot in the vertex stage): 4 alpha-mask and 60 shadow draw calls, median 9 ms; the crowns still sway.
