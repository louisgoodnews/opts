## 🎯 Objective

Minimize AI output token payloads (`edit_file` block sizes) by decomposing the **191 production functions** over 80 lines of code into focused ≤ 40-line helpers and replacing the **155 `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments | clippy::type_complexity)]`** annotations across 113 files with cohesive `#[derive(SystemParam)]` structs.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

*Progress 2026-10-29 (`agent/registry-split`, second slice):* the five settings-registry builders (`graphics`, `gameplay`, `interface`, `content`, the lighting `descriptors`) are split into groups of at most about 75 lines; functions over 80 lines 171 → 166. Verified differently from the first batch, because that one needed an audit to find silent changes: the full `Debug` output of every descriptor (4,580 lines) was dumped before and after and is **byte-identical**, and the generated `settings.md` stays fresh. The same method fits any declarative table; it does not fit Bevy systems, which need a test per behaviour.

*Progress 2026-10-29 (`agent/m0150-functions`, first batch):* functions over 80 lines 249 → 172 (production 198 → 128; production over 200 lines 26 → 14; 121–200 lines 60 → 38; tests/examples 51 → 44) and signature allows 145 → 112, with new `#[derive(SystemParam)]` bundles in the mirror systems and `opts-gui` (combobox, focus, handlers, tooltip, master–detail, theme) and the other allows dropped where systems were split. Among them `vehicle_callbacks_with_context`, `bus::parse`, `o3d_parts_core`, the `.hof`/tile/`.sli`/`.sco` parsers and `discover`. `docs/dev/function-known.txt` reseeded (376 → 271 lines); no regressions. A line-by-line audit against master before the merge found and undid behaviour changes the uncommitted refactor had slipped in (changed constants, rewritten algorithms and output, invented plural rules, lost docs); test and example files it had rewritten were restored to master.

3. `./scripts/local-ci.sh` passes with zero behavioural change.
