## 🎯 Objective

Minimize AI output token payloads (`edit_file` block sizes) by decomposing the **191 production functions** over 80 lines of code into focused ≤ 40-line helpers and replacing the **155 `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments | clippy::type_complexity)]`** annotations across 113 files with cohesive `#[derive(SystemParam)]` structs.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [ ] D1 Decompose the 25 production functions over 200 lines of code (e.g., `vehicle_callbacks_with_context`, `spawn_traffic`, `bus::parse`, `o3d_parts_core`, `load_segment_meshes_for`, `menu_actions`) into ≤ 40-line helpers.
- [ ] D2 Decompose the 55 production functions in the 121–200 line band across `opts-game`, `opts-bevy`, `opts-omsi`, and `opts-sim`.
- [ ] D3 Decompose the remaining 111 production functions in the 81–120 line band (and 51 oversized test/example functions) until `docs/dev/function-known.txt` is empty.
- [ ] D4 Bundle multi-query/resource Bevy system parameters into `#[derive(SystemParam)]` structs across `opts-game` (94 allows), `opts-bevy` (49 allows), `opts-gui` (8 allows), `opts-sim` (2 allows), and `opts-benchmark` (2 allows), removing the `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments)]` and `#[allow(clippy::type_complexity)]` attributes.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. `python3 scripts/structure_audit.py check` reports **0 functions over 80 lines of code** (`docs/dev/function-known.txt` is empty).
2. All unjustifiable `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments)]` and `#[allow(clippy::type_complexity)]` annotations are replaced by `SystemParam` structs or parameter structs.
3. `./scripts/local-ci.sh` passes with zero behavioural change.
