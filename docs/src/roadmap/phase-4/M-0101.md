## 🎯 Objective

The scripts' door logic, passenger requests and ticket machine work with a passenger system: people board through doors that the scripts open, tickets are sold and change is given through the scripts' cash logic.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** `PAX_*` variables, `humans_count`, `GetHumanCountOn*`, cabin path graph, tickets and money.

**Out of scope:** Pedestrian AI beyond boarding, cash desk graphics beyond what the model provides.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. Passengers enter only through open doors; no passenger inside a closed door.
2. HH109: a ticket sale gives the change the script computes; `GivenTicket` and the cash events are consistent.
3. `GetHumanCountOn*` equals the number of passengers placed in a scripted scenario.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

### Evidence check (ISS-027, 2026-11-22)

Read from the test names and bodies; the tests were not run on this date. Paths are under `crates/`.

- **Doors and boarding:** evidenced. `opts-sim/src/passengers/tests/boarding.rs::a_passenger_boards_only_through_an_open_door`; `opts-game/src/passengers/system/doors/tests.rs::door_flags_decide_boarding`.
- **Requests `PAX_*_Req`:** evidenced. `entry_requests` and `exit_requests` are asserted in `opts-sim/src/passengers/tests/boarding.rs` and `journey.rs`; the scripts hear their own requests in `opts-bevy/tests/consist_scripts.rs::a_part_hears_its_own_door_requests_and_not_the_heads`.
- **Alighting at stops:** evidenced. `opts-sim/src/passengers/tests/journey.rs::at_the_stop_those_who_are_done_walk_to_the_exit_and_get_off_when_it_opens`; a unit behind gets off through its own door only when that opens (`tests_trailer.rs`).
- **Stops from `[busstop]`:** evidenced. `opts-bevy/src/bus_stops.rs` (two tests).

- **Tickets:** the `.otp` parser is tested (`opts-omsi/src/formats/ticketpack/tests.rs`); `GetTicketValue`, `GetTicketName` and `GiveChangeCoin` are tested in `opts-bevy/src/host_callbacks/tests/tickets.rs`. **Not evidenced:** `GivenTicket`, and the HH109 change (acceptance 2).
