## 🎯 Objective

The scripts' door logic, passenger requests and ticket machine work with a passenger system: people board through doors that the scripts open, tickets are sold and change is given through the scripts' cash logic.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** `PAX_*` variables, `humans_count`, `GetHumanCountOn*`, cabin path graph, tickets and money.

**Out of scope:** Pedestrian AI beyond boarding, cash desk graphics beyond what the model provides.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [ ] Door state from `PAX_Entry*/Exit*_Open`; requests `PAX_*_Req` from waiting passengers; `humans_count`; `GetHumanCountOnSeat` and `GetHumanCountOnPathLink` over the `passengercabin.cfg` and `paths.cfg` graph (chain-wide on consists).
- [ ] Boarding and alighting flows at stops driven by door state.
- [ ] Tickets: `TicketPacks/*.otp` and `Money/*.cfg` parsers; `GetTicketValue`, `GetTicketName`, `GiveChangeCoin`; `GivenTicket`; cash desk interaction (UI).
- [ ] Docs: `passengercabin_cfg.md`, `paths_cfg.md` updated; the index space of the human-count callbacks documented after experiment E6.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. Passengers enter only through open doors; no passenger inside a closed door.
2. HH109: a ticket sale gives the change the script computes; `GivenTicket` and the cash events are consistent.
3. `GetHumanCountOn*` equals the number of passengers placed in a scripted scenario.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- Passenger index space is inferred (E6): keep it behind one adapter.
- Performance with full buses: counts are cached per frame.
