## 🎯 Objective

AI vehicles are heard: engine idle and revs by speed, brakes, horns and doors where the vehicle files say so, with range and voice budget that keep a busy street affordable.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [ ] Docs first: the `[sound_ai]` file of a `.bus` (the lighter AI set) and the AI vehicles of `Vehicles` that carry sound configurations; census of the engine sample families (names with the `@RPM` reference, 1,312 referenced names), their loop points and the parameters that map speed to pitch and level.
- [ ] A shared AI sound set per vehicle model (one clip decode for all instances of the model) and an emitter per AI vehicle, positioned on it; `speed` and `gain` from its speed and acceleration (the AI model variables of `ai_variables`).
- [ ] Voice management for traffic: far vehicles are virtual, the nearest few carry the voices; the budget is shared with M-0139.
- [ ] Events: braking squeal, horn, door opening where the AI file defines them.
- [ ] Tests without a sound card (levels from distance and speed, budget under 200 vehicles); live check with traffic on a busy map.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
1. On a map with traffic, a bus passing the camera is heard to approach, pass (stereo pan) and recede; `audio.state` shows the AI emitters with their computed levels.
2. 200 AI vehicles do not exceed the voice budget and the frame time does not rise measurably.
3. `./scripts/local-ci.sh` passes; no OMSI asset committed.
