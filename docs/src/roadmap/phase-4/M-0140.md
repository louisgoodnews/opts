## 🎯 Objective

AI vehicles are heard: engine idle and revs by speed, brakes, horns and doors where the vehicle files say so, with range and voice budget that keep a busy street affordable.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks
- [x] Docs first (done: [sound_cfg](../../formats/text/vehicles/sound_cfg.md) "Sound configurations of AI vehicles"; 499 configurations, 45,043 definitions, 6,319 playable with the variables the traffic model states): the `[sound_ai]` file of a `.bus` (the lighter AI set) and the AI vehicles of `Vehicles` that carry sound configurations; census of the engine sample families (names with the `@RPM` reference, 1,312 referenced names), their loop points and the parameters that map speed to pitch and level.
- [x] A sound set per vehicle type (`AiSoundSet`, chosen when the type loads, shared by all instances; decoded clips are shared through the `ClipLoader`) and voices per AI vehicle (one clip decode for all instances of the model) and an emitter per AI vehicle, positioned on it; `speed` and `gain` from its speed and acceleration (the AI model variables of `ai_variables`).
- [x] Voice management for traffic (distance, the engine's voice budget shared with M-0139; at most 16 voices per vehicle): far vehicles are virtual, the nearest few carry the voices; the budget is shared with M-0139.
- [x] Events: the horn (`ev_AI_Horn`, raised by drivers who are stuck) and the door sounds of scheduled buses (their scripts' events and `door_N` variables, the player's sound machinery); open: braking squeal.
- [~] Tests without a sound card (done: variables, levels, pitch, gear model, selection, corpus gate over 499 configurations); live check on Berlin-Spandau with 60 vehicles: with the camera among the traffic 4 to 8 voices are audible and have sinks, the level of an engine or rolling loop rises from 0.05 at 30 m to 0.16 at 6 m (levels from distance and speed, budget under 200 vehicles); live check with traffic on a busy map.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
1. On a map with traffic, a bus passing the camera is heard to approach, pass (stereo pan) and recede; `audio.state` shows the AI emitters with their computed levels.
2. 200 AI vehicles do not exceed the voice budget and the frame time does not rise measurably.
3. `./scripts/local-ci.sh` passes; no OMSI asset committed.
