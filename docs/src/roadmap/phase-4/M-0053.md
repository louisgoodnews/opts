## 🎯 Objective

Finish what the 2026-10-11 articulation work started:
- physics and rendering agree on one articulation model;
- segments follow the terrain (pitch, so `articulation_<n>_beta` gets a value);
- `[couple_front]` push-pull units and trailers of AI and parked vehicles load.
