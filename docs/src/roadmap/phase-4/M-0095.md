## 🎯 Objective

AI vehicles behave according to their own files. A 0.8 t car, a 15 t lorry and a 15 km/h scooter accelerate, brake, follow and top out differently; articulated and other non-car vehicles of the map's AI lists appear; rail and air vehicles never drive on roads. Today every AI vehicle uses one of two hard-coded IDM parameter sets at about 50 km/h (investigation: 110 spawns of 14 types, desired speed 45-55 km/h for all).

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** the vehicle file → traffic model hand-over (`AiProfile`, `VehicleTraits`, `IdmParams::from_traits`), use of all groups of `ailists.cfg`, class and domain from `[ai_veh_type]` and `[type]`, trailer chains for `[couple_back]`, animation inputs from axle data, the parser fields this needs (`[ai_veh_type]`, `[ai_brakeperformance]`, `[rowdy_factor]`, `[ai_deltaheight]`, and the four unread player keywords `[rollwiderstand]`, `[inv_min_turnradius]`, `[schwerpunkt]`, `[rot_pnt_long]`).

**Out of scope:** the scripted dynamic mode (M-0049 steps 4-7), curve speed limits, rebalancing the IDM for "feel", lane graph, signals, right of way, where and when vehicles spawn, scheduled AI, the player simulation (including its 0.5 t mass filter, noted as a follow-up).

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. **Runtime comparison.** Two agents on the same straight lane from rest, one with the profile of a light car (about 0.8-1.0 t) and one with a 15 t lorry's real constants: the car reaches 40 km/h at least 30 % sooner than the lorry, and from 50 km/h the stopping distances differ in the order of `AI_F_brems_max / [mass]` of the two files. The same test fails when `from_traits` is replaced by the old two-set choice (negative control).
2. **Declared speeds are respected.** In a headless run, no vehicle's logged desired speed exceeds its `AI_v_max` or its lane's limit, and a vehicle with `AI_v_max` 15 km/h (the HamburgLi20 scooters) is logged at no more than 15 km/h. Per-type desired speeds differ between types in the log.
3. **Mix.** On a map with several AI groups, vehicles from more than one group are spawned in a run of two minutes; no `[type]` 2/3 vehicle appears on a road lane.
4. **Chains.** On a map whose list has a `[couple_back]` vehicle, a head with its trailer follows the lane; the trailer yaw follows the kinematic rule and both units despawn together.
5. **Corpus.** The profile test passes over all AI-group vehicles of all installed maps; only the listed fallbacks occur.
6. `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features` and `./scripts/local-ci.sh` pass; no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

## ⚠️ Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| The mapping from `AI_M_gas_max` and friends to IDM numbers is a formula built on an inferred script law (❓) | Clamped formulas with named calibration constants; phase 2 of the design measures the real script response through the script VM; calibration against OMSI stays Q-AI01 |
| Heavier vehicles and chains raise the per-frame cost | Existing vehicle cap and per-group density; measure on a dense map before enabling by default |
| Declared constants of some vehicles are missing or zero | Fallback to today's parameters, counted by the corpus test, never a panic |
| `[type]` 2 on a road scooter contradicts the documentation | Domain filter considers `[ai_veh_type]` and the group first; log the case; clarify in the format docs |

## 📝 Implementation notes (2026-10-14)

**Where the code is.** `opts_core::formats::vehicles::{ai_profile, bus::VehicleExtras}`; `opts_traffic::traits` (`VehicleTraits`, `Calibration`, `IdmParams::from_traits`); `opts-game/src/in_game/omsi_world/{traffic_variant, traffic_chain, traffic}.rs`.

**Acceptance results.**

1. *Runtime comparison* (`crates/opts-traffic/tests/vehicle_traits.rs`, real declared constants: car 1.3 t, drive 1000, brake 18,000 N; bus 14 t, drive 8000, brake 80,000 N): 0 to 40 km/h takes **9.5 s for the car and 19.6 s for the bus**; braking for a parked vehicle from 50 km/h takes **62.3 m for the car and 69.1 m for the bus** (the bus starts earlier and brakes more gently); the negative control with the old single parameter set gives identical times for both.
2. *Declared speeds.* The desired speed is capped by the vehicle's `AI_v_max` and the lane's limit by construction (unit tests `top_speed_and_lane_limit_cap_the_desired_speed` and `a_slow_vehicle_keeps_to_its_declared_top_speed`). Headless runs: per-vehicle parameters now differ (Waldhofen 115 spawns of 13 types: acceleration 0.53-2.55 m/s², comfortable braking 1.0-3.0 m/s²; Lucerne 231 spawns: a 14 t `Citybus_L_AI` at 0.67 m/s² and 5.7 m/s² hardest braking, class `Bus`, against 1.2 t hatchbacks at 0.6-0.7 and 8.3). **Not observed live:** the 15 km/h scooters of HamburgLi20; neither the old nor the new binary spawned any vehicle on that map within five minutes of a headless run (a map-specific loading or placement issue that predates this change), so the scooter is covered by the unit tests only.
3. *Mix.* Aachen Linie 33 now spawns from `NormalCars` and `Trucks` (an `Atego` of 2.5 t at 0.53 m/s², class `Truck`); Rheinhausen picks four groups (`NormalCars`, `Commercials`, `SpecialCars`, `Trucks`) but did not reach spawning in a headless run of eight minutes (same loading issue as above). No `[type]` 2/3 vehicle is drawn from a participating group of the installed maps; rail and air vehicles are refused in `load_variant` (unit-tested through `AiProfile::domain`).
4. *Chains.* **No participating group of any installed map contains a `[couple_back]` vehicle** (the articulated buses and trains sit in groups that `unsched_vehgroups.txt` leaves at density 0 or does not list: they belong to scheduled traffic, which is out of scope). The chain is therefore verified by system tests (`traffic_chain.rs`: the trailer hangs behind the lead at the coupling, becomes visible, its joint stays on the hitch within 5 cm through a 15 m-radius turn and it swings behind the lead) and not by a run on a stock map.
5. *Corpus.* `ai_profile_corpus` builds a profile for all 426 distinct `.bus`/`.ovh` vehicles of the AI groups of the 45 maps (0 unparsable, 2 files missing). Fallbacks: mass 6, brake force 33, drive torque 60, top speed 109, wheel radius 0; 22 rail and 6 air vehicles are recognised; 27 vehicles declare a trailer; `[ai_veh_type]` is present on 50 of them.
6. Gates: see the commit log; no unsafe code, no OMSI asset committed.

**Behaviour changes to know about.** Vehicles that declare `[rowdy_factor]` now follow their declared range: the calmest (`0 0`, 91 files) drive at 90 % of the open-road speed (45 km/h) instead of a random 90-110 %, and `0 0.5` (150 files) at 90-100 %; vehicles without it keep the old 90-110 % spread. The mean speed of traffic on maps made of such vehicles is therefore a few km/h lower than before (Aachen median 45 km/h); whether OMSI's rowdy factor means this is Q-AIC-002. Vehicles whose constants are missing keep the old class defaults.

**Open.** Q-AIC-001 to Q-AIC-006 of the design stand. Size rules: `traffic.rs` (841 lines) and `bus.rs` (784) were over the 500-line limit before and still are; the new code lives in files under 400 lines.
