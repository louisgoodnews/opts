## 🎯 Objective

AI vehicles behave according to their own files. A 0.8 t car, a 15 t lorry and a 15 km/h scooter accelerate, brake, follow and top out differently; articulated and other non-car vehicles of the map's AI lists appear; rail and air vehicles never drive on roads. Today every AI vehicle uses one of two hard-coded IDM parameter sets at about 50 km/h (investigation: 110 spawns of 14 types, desired speed 45-55 km/h for all).

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** the vehicle file → traffic model hand-over (`AiProfile`, `VehicleTraits`, `IdmParams::from_traits`), use of all groups of `ailists.cfg`, class and domain from `[ai_veh_type]` and `[type]`, trailer chains for `[couple_back]`, animation inputs from axle data, the parser fields this needs (`[ai_veh_type]`, `[ai_brakeperformance]`, `[rowdy_factor]`, `[ai_deltaheight]`, and the four unread player keywords `[rollwiderstand]`, `[inv_min_turnradius]`, `[schwerpunkt]`, `[rot_pnt_long]`).

**Out of scope:** the scripted dynamic mode (M-0049 steps 4-7), curve speed limits, rebalancing the IDM for "feel", lane graph, signals, right of way, where and when vehicles spawn, scheduled AI, the player simulation (including its 0.5 t mass filter, noted as a follow-up).

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. **Runtime comparison.** Two agents on the same straight lane from rest, one with the profile of a light car (about 0.8-1.0 t) and one with a 15 t lorry's real constants: the car reaches 40 km/h at least 30 % sooner than the lorry, and from 50 km/h the stopping distances differ in the order of `AI_F_brems_max / [mass]` of the two files. The same test fails when `from_traits` is replaced by the old two-set choice (negative control).
2. **Declared speeds are respected.** In a headless run, no vehicle's logged desired speed exceeds its `AI_v_max` or its lane's limit, and a vehicle with `AI_v_max` 15 km/h (the HamburgLi20 scooters) is logged at no more than 15 km/h. Per-type desired speeds differ between types in the log.
3. **Mix.** On a map with several AI groups, vehicles from more than one group are spawned in a run of two minutes; no `[type]` 2/3 vehicle appears on a road lane.
4. **Chains.** On a map whose list has a `[couple_back]` vehicle, a head with its trailer follows the lane; the trailer yaw follows the kinematic rule and both units despawn together.
5. **Corpus.** The profile test passes over all AI-group vehicles of all installed maps; only the listed fallbacks occur.
6. `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features` and `./scripts/local-ci.sh` pass; no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

## ⚠️ Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| The mapping from `AI_M_gas_max` and friends to IDM numbers is a formula built on an inferred script law (❓) | Clamped formulas with named calibration constants; phase 2 of the design measures the real script response through the script VM; calibration against OMSI stays Q-AI01 |
| Heavier vehicles and chains raise the per-frame cost | Existing vehicle cap and per-group density; measure on a dense map before enabling by default |
| Declared constants of some vehicles are missing or zero | Fallback to today's parameters, counted by the corpus test, never a panic |
| `[type]` 2 on a road scooter contradicts the documentation | Domain filter considers `[ai_veh_type]` and the group first; log the case; clarify in the format docs |
