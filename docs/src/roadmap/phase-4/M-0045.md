## 🎯 Objective

Make the running scripts visible: variables the scripts write move meshes (`[newanim]`), switch meshes (`[visible]`), light lamps and open doors, and the player can raise the triggers (keys and cockpit hot-spots) that start those scripts.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In Scope:**
- Research task first: the axis convention of `anim_rot`/`anim_trans` (rotation about which axis of the origin frame, sign, units) and the order of several `[newanim]` blocks — decide by comparing OPTS and OMSI screenshots of the same variable values (running OMSI for screenshots is permitted) and record the result in `model_cfg.md`.
- `ScriptAnimation` component and a PostUpdate system applying `anim_rot`/`anim_trans` (`delay`, `maxspeed` smoothing) and `[visible]` from engine variables.
- Remaining predefined variables fed by the simulation (wheel speeds, axle suspension, door and passenger state stubs) so the stock scripts reach their normal state.
- Lights: `[matl_lightmap]`/`[matl_nightmap]` and `[interiorlight]`/`[spotlight]` driven by variables; indicators and brake lights from the player's inputs.
- Triggers: key bindings (`name`, `name_off`) and mesh hot-spots (`[mouseevent]`: `mesh`, `mesh_drag`, `mesh_off`) call `ScriptEngine::trigger`.
- Script events (`drain_events`) delivered to the audio layer when M-0027 exists; until then logged at `debug`.

**Out of Scope:**
- Letting `M_Wheel` from the engine/gearbox scripts replace the kinematic drivetrain (separate decision after the physics comparison).
