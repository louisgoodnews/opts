## 🎯 Objective

Destination boards, IBIS and matrix displays are drawn by the scripts' own `ST*` calls into canvases the model binds to meshes.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** `ST*` callbacks, `GetFontIndex`, `TextLength`, `[scripttexture]`, `[useScriptTexture]`, `\S:n`, font registry, upload.

**Out of scope:** Route data (M-0099), `[texttexture]` (exists).

## ✍️ Findings 2026-10-21

* The board entry was stale: most of the deliverables were already in the code. The control command `vehicle.script_textures {prefix?}` (protocol 1.26) writes every canvas of the loaded vehicles as a PNG, and `vehicle.script_vars` now reports which callbacks the scripts called (`callbacks.real`/`stub`).
* On the NLC the matrix scripts draw nothing until a route is selected (`bus.set_route`); then about 49,000 `STDrawRect` and 10 `STTextOut` calls per second arrive. `STTextOut` filled every glyph cell with the current colour (black boxes). It now blends the glyph cells of the font's colour bitmap through its alpha bitmap (`opts_bevy::script_text`), advancing by the cell width plus the spacing argument. The colour of the text is the font's own (the matrix fonts keep their LED colour there); whether `STSetColor` tints text is open (E3), as is the meaning of the alignment argument (E2: left aligned is what runs).
* What the NLC draws after a route is chosen reads `K 0000000.0` in two lines: a placeholder string of its own, not the destination; the cause (the string callbacks behind it) is not yet found.
* **Same day, second round.** The NLC's matrix is not drawn by `Hanover`: `VMatrix.osc` rasterises the text into slot 0 (256 x 64, never shown), reads it back pixel by pixel (`STReadPixel`/`STGetR`) and draws the LEDs as alpha masks into slots 1 to 5 (the `\S:n` of `[matl_transmap]`). Three host defects came out of that: `STTextOut` must draw in the colour set with `STSetColor`, with the font's alpha as coverage on every channel (the matrix sets alpha 0 and red 255 and reads the red channel), `STLoadTex` paths are Windows paths relative to the vehicle's texture folder (`..\..\Anzeigen\Krueger\...`: backslashes, any letter case; they never resolved on Linux), and a loaded bitmap goes to the top left at its own size (no scaling, the script reads pixels at fixed places). The printer that selects the destination did not start because `{if}` popped its condition (see the VM sheet); `bus.set_route` now presses its keys.
* **Still open on the NLC:** with the route 132-01 chosen the matrix canvas shows the line number but not the destination text, and the yard's palette and destination bitmaps do not exist in the installation (`Anzeigen/Krueger` has none for Ahlheim), so the front board stays dark. `vehicle.script_textures` now writes every slot of every unit (CPU side) to look at it.
* **Third round (destination text and the sun blind).** The NLC's destination text was lost by two string operators: `$length` took its string off the stack (the matrix script measures a string and keeps working with it) and `$StrToFloat` answered 0 for text, so `Matrix_ProcessGoTo` took every plain destination for a `*GOTO[n]` command and swallowed it. Both are fixed (`$length` peeks, non-numbers give -1; checked on the NLC, NG313, Urbino, C2, O530 and NF6D by dumping every script variable: only timers and per-vehicle random numbers differ). With the route 132-01 the LED mask (slot 5) now holds `12` and `Rosental Bf`. A canvas used as `[matl_transmap] \S:n` now serves as the alpha of the material's own colour (`VehicleScriptTexture.tint`, the average of the colour texture's bright pixels).
* **Still open:** the board shows nothing yet. The colour texture of that mesh is named by the string variable `Matrix_Farbe` (`[matl_freetex]`), which the tint does not follow, and the LED meshes' visibility variables and light maps were not checked.
* **Sun blind of the NLC** (`Rollo_v`): its scissor arms turned the fabric out of the bus, a grey panel hanging in front of the destination board. Their rotation sense is now reversed for the y/z-swapped arm meshes (like the door chains); the fabric no longer shows outside, and deployed (`Rollo_v` 1) it lies under the roof lining behind the windscreen. Not checked against the original, and the side blind (matrix-rotated meshes) is untouched.
* **Fourth round (the board shows its text).** What looked like a dark board was timing: the matrix draws its LEDs progressively (pixel refresh), and the screenshots were taken after 40 s; after about 90 s the front board of the NLC reads `132` and `Düsseldorf Str. / Fachhochschule` (route 132-01), faintly, in grey. `vehicle.script_textures` reports `on_meshes` (per bound image the slot, its tint and the number of pixels with alpha) and the images reach the five LED layers of `gelenk_v\Matrix.o3d` (blended parts, light map on `elec_busbar_main`). Canvas images are uploaded as complete images with their mip chains (replacing only the data left the other mip levels empty), the tint follows the colour texture named by `Matrix_Farbe` (`[matl_freetex]`; orange here, `[220, 111, 0]`) and LED layers are drawn unlit.
* **Fifth round (LED boards).** The LED layers are lit and amber also from a distance: LED canvases are uploaded without a mip chain and sampled linearly (`opts_bevy::text_texture::led_image`; the mip chain averaged each lit dot into the dark between the dots), ignore the `.o3d` diffuse alpha (it dimmed them a second time) and use a small depth bias. At 9.6 m the NLC's board reads `132` and `Düsseldorf Str. / Fachhochschule` in amber.
* **NF6D (checked).** Its matrix is fed by the tram's own IBIS (`IBIS_setmode_linie_kurs`, `IBIS_Eingabe`, the digits), which accepts keys only while the cab is active (`SLR_A_Kopf_Inaktiv` not 1) and while no scheduled target is set (`AI_target_index` 0, `SetLineTo` empty). `bus.set_route` used to hand over the target first, which shut the keypad; now the keys are typed first and the target is offered when they are done. With the cab active (`kw_s_N`) route 107-01 gives `Matrix_Nr` `107`, `Matrix_TerminusL1/L2` and the side boards of the cars show the line and destination (looked at from outside).
* **Not checked:** the NG313's board (the Bremen depot has no routes for it: `bus.routes` is empty), the HH109's flip-dot matrix (checked 2026-10-26, see below), and the other LED variants (slots 1 to 4: green, azure, magenta).
* **Fonts with differently sized bitmaps (2026-10-25).** 50 of the corpus's 1,108 fonts have an alpha bitmap of another size than the colour bitmap (printer and IBIS fonts: a black 128×128 colour bitmap with a 128×200 or 512×512 alpha bitmap). The `.oft` cells are pixel positions in both, but the coverage was read at positions scaled to the colour bitmap, and glyph rows below the colour bitmap were skipped: the MAN printer's `IBIS-2_5x7` drew the `j` into every space and garbled the digits. Coverage is now read at the cell's own pixels (`FontBitmaps::coverage`), glyphs are bounded by either bitmap, and the colour bitmap is read only where it exists; the MAN NL printer reads `12:00` (checked in the game).
* **Vehicle text textures keep their mip chain (2026-10-25).** A redrawn text replaced only the image data, so the smaller mip levels of the first (blank) image stayed empty and text drawn small vanished; the image is now rebuilt (`text_image`), as the canvases are.
* **Redrawn text reaches the GPU (2026-10-25, `agent/efad-text`).** The HH109 EFAD printer's text lines did not show on its LCD although the images were right. Two Bevy 0.20 behaviours: (1) a modified image of the same size and format is copied into the texture already on the GPU, and that copy (`write_texture`) fills only mip level 0, so the smaller levels kept the first, blank picture: text seen small (a 1024 × 64 line on a few centimetres of LCD, sampled from a small mip) stayed empty while the MAN printer, seen nearly full size, worked; (2) a material's bind group keeps the texture it was made with, so a redrawn image that does get a new texture is not seen until the material is prepared again. Fix: mipmapped text images drop `COPY_DST` (each redraw gets a new texture with its whole chain) and `rebind_redrawn_images` marks the materials of every redrawn text texture or script canvas modified (`RedrawnImages`). Note: `Assets::get_mut` alone does not mark an asset modified in 0.20, only a mutable dereference or `into_inner` does. Checked in the game: the EFAD reads `0`, `ZL`, the time and `verkaufsbereit` at night; the C2, S31x, Lion's City and HH109 VHH cockpits unchanged. The EFAD time's sixth digit (`22:005`) is **the script's own**: `Ticketprinter_efad.osc` appends a tenth-of-a-minute digit after `HH:MM` and cuts the string to six characters (`6 $SetLengthL`); the clock mesh shows pixels 470–557 of the 1024-pixel texture, where the centred six characters (11 + 1 px each, `EFADfont`) fit, so OMSI shows it too unless its `$SetLengthL` or layout differs from ours (❓, the add-on manual's photo is too small to tell; checked 2026-10-25).
* **MB C2 BVG display line (checked 2026-10-26, `agent/c2-display`).** The values of the instrument display (`Display_Text`, e.g. `12:00   15`: time and outside temperature) render correctly (font `C2_Display_Mx200`, left aligned, white; checked offline with `render_text_texture`, whose PNG output was fixed for the mip chain). The `.o3d` material is self-illuminating (emissive 1, 1, 1), which OPTS applies. The quad (`Dashboard\Display\Display_Text.o3d`, 2.2 × 0.5 cm) lies at the bottom edge of the display and does not show from the default eye point, even when drawn on top; the white box below the speedometer is the odometer line (`Dashboard_Text_Step2`). Open ❓: whether it is the steering-wheel rim, the `[visible] Tankleiste` condition at that moment, or the placement; compare with OMSI.
* **MAN Lion's City VDV page (2026-10-26, `agent/vdv-dim`).** The "dim" stop-mode page was two bugs. (1) The display stacks a background (`model\vdv\BHD_LC_VDV_Display.o3d`, placeholder texture, opaque) and the page with its overlays (`model\vdv\Display\BHD_LC_VDV_Display.o3d`, ten copies) on one screen; OMSI draws meshes in file order and a later surface at equal depth wins, Bevy z-fought and showed the placeholder. Later meshes of the same file name now get a slightly larger depth bias (`segments.rs::in_file_order`). (2) The page's `.o3d` material is self-illuminating (emissive 1, 1, 1): in OMSI that lights the texture (the green LCD at full brightness), in Bevy it was a flat white added on top. A textured material's emissive now uses its texture (`material::emission_texture`; glass splits and free textures carry it along). Checked live: the page shows its bus outline, frame and markers; four cockpits and a night city scene unchanged otherwise. The C2 odometer box is unaffected (not emissive).
* **NG272 and HH109 destination boards (2026-10-26, `agent/boards`).** Four defects. (1) *Depot choice:* the NG272 got `Berlin.hof` (destinations, no routes) before `Spandau.hof`, so its board stayed empty; among depots named after the map the one with the most routes now wins. The HH109 on `Hamburg109` (friendly name `Hamburg Tag & Nacht`) matched no depot name at all and got the first file of its folder (`000_S-Bahn-Berlin.hof`); `find_depot` now takes the map's friendly and folder names and, failing a name match, compares tokens: shared numbers, then shared words, then tokens of the vehicle's folder (the operator `HHA`), then the fewest extra tokens, so `1_HH109_HHA.hof` wins over `-2`, `-VAN`, `VHHPVG` and `Stadtrundfahrt Hamburg`. OMSI lets the player pick the depot; this is OPTS's default. (2) *`STFilter` blurred:* OPTS ran a 3 × 3 box blur, and `VMatrix.osc` calls `STFilter` after every progressive refresh, so the blur compounded and only the newest dot row stayed sharp. It now leaves the pixels unchanged (a sampling flag, as the runtime design assumed ❓). The scattered dots stuck on or off are the script's own: `Matrix_Fehlerpixel` grows with the bus's age (`Matrix_Baujahr` 1992, 0.001 per year) and `NrSpecRandom` picks them. (3) *The font gap:* `.oft` cells are inked edge to edge and `[newfont]`'s fifth line is the gap between glyphs; text textures ignored it, so the HH109's line number (`#33` plus 43 spaces, `krueger-font_linie`, gap 5) was centred out of its window (texture pixels 0–105 of 512). With the gap the board reads `33 U-ALSTERDORF`, and other text has its letter spacing (`DIN Narrow`). `TextLength` keeps the cells without the gap: with it the NG272's `STADTGRENZE` came out cramped ❓. (4) Checked in the game: NG272 `92 HEERSTR. / STADTGRENZE`, HH109 `33 U-ALSTERDORF`. **Open ❓:** the HH109's big destination text (hof string 1, `krueger-font_gross`, padded with up to 47 spaces) is 600–700 px wide in the 512 px texture the board shows from 105 to 390; no rule tried (centre, centre clamped, left, with or without the gap) places it inside, so OMSI's overflow handling is unknown. It appears with the `1_HH109-2_*` depots.
* **ATRON AFR4 screen (2026-10-26, `agent/open-items`, BRT C2).** The screen showed its page pictures but none of its text (inputs, clock, date). Two defects. (1) The script draws its text with `STSetColor` alpha 0, red 255, and the model shows canvases 10/16 as `[matl_transmap] \S:10` over the page picture: OPTS gave the glyphs that alpha, so the transparency map stayed empty. Glyphs drawn by `STTextOut` are now opaque whatever alpha was set (their colour channels as before; the matrix scripts read red, and `STDrawRect` keeps the set alpha for the LED dots). (2) The text's colour comes from the texture `[matl_freetex]` names (`AFR4\1\ST_2.bmp`, black under the fields, white under the clock); the mesh's own texture is a black placeholder without bright pixels, so no tint was found and the canvas never followed the free texture. A transparency-map canvas now follows its free texture whatever its first tint, and a colour texture of the canvas's proportions (at least half its size) colours each pixel (`ColourMap`), while small swatches (LED textures) keep the average tint. Checked: card in the slot (`AFR4_Karte_2`), seller 4951 and PIN show in black, clock and date in black; the AFR4 goes on to *Linie/Kurs*; the BRT C2's instrument display shows its values too; NG272 and NLC boards unchanged. How to get past the start page: the driver card must go into the slot (click the slot, not the card), and a field is typed into after its ▶ is pressed.
