## 🎯 Objective

Destination boards, IBIS and matrix displays are drawn by the scripts' own `ST*` calls into canvases the model binds to meshes.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** `ST*` callbacks, `GetFontIndex`, `TextLength`, `[scripttexture]`, `[useScriptTexture]`, `\S:n`, font registry, upload.

**Out of scope:** Route data (M-0099), `[texttexture]` (exists).

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [ ] `CanvasService`: a CPU canvas per `[scripttexture]` of the part running the script; `STNewTex`, `STLock`, `STUnlock`, `STFilter`, `STSetColor`, `STDrawRect`, `STTextOut`, `STReadPixel`, `STGetR/G/B`, `STLoadTex`; the three never-called calls registered as stubs.
- [ ] Font registry from `Fonts/*.oft` keyed by `[newfont]` name; `GetFontIndex`, `TextLength` from the glyph advances.
- [ ] `model.cfg`: parse `[scripttexture]` fully, `[useScriptTexture] n` and `\S:n` material names; bind canvases to meshes.
- [ ] Upload of a dirty canvas as a Bevy `Image` at most once per frame per canvas and within a per-frame budget.
- [ ] Defaults for the open arguments: `STTextOut` `a`, `b` = 0 behaviour exact, others logged; `STSetColor` order `tex, A, R, G, B`; flags to switch once experiments E2/E3 are known.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. Destination board of SD200, HH109 and NF6D rendered; text layout equals the `TextLength` arithmetic of the script (centering test); golden images committed.
2. At most 0.5 ms per frame with 8 canvases; no per-frame allocation growth over 10,000 frames.
3. Corpus: the integration report shows 0 stubbed `ST*` calls for all 936 buses that use them.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- Unverified argument semantics (E2-E5): logged and switchable.
- Large matrices redraw every frame: dirty-rect upload and budget.
