## 🎯 Objective

Background road traffic on OMSI maps: vehicles from the map's AI lists drive along the road network, keep their distance, obey signals and priority, and appear and disappear around the player according to the map's density curves.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** unscheduled road traffic (cars, lorries, cyclists, ordinary buses) — lane graph, car following, spawning and density, signals, speed limits and priority rules, two fidelity modes (kinematic and script-driven), debug tooling.

**Out of scope:** scheduled bus/tram AI (timetables, stops, depots), pedestrians, rail signalling, lane changes/overtaking, collisions with the player (hooks are listed in the plan, §8).

## 📝 Notes

* The AI "driver" only produces pedal and indicator values; OMSI's own AI vehicle scripts turn them into motion (`M_Wheel = Throttle · AI_M_gas_max · (1 − v / AI_v_max_erreich)`). That is why dynamic mode reuses the player-vehicle machinery unchanged.
* OMSI's AI algorithm is undocumented; IDM defaults are literature values, not OMSI measurements, until Phase F.
