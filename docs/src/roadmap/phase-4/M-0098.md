## 🎯 Objective

A coupled vehicle (articulated bus, tram, train) is one logical vehicle with one script state: every part runs its own script set, parts with `[scriptshare]` share the previous part's store, control cables copy state between parts, triggers reach the part that owns the binding.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** `VehicleScriptHost` per consist, `[scriptshare]`, `[control_cable_*]`, `[couple_front]`, trigger routing, single writer for `articulation_*`, rail on its scripts' torque.

**Out of scope:** Consist mass dynamics (M-0053), passenger flow (M-0101).

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [x] `ScriptConsist` on the head and an engine per unit with its own scripts (`ScriptPart`), stepped after the head front to back (`opts-bevy::consist_scripts`; no separate `VehicleScriptHost` type).
- [x] Parse `[control_cable_front/back]` (`ControlCable`); the bridge (`opts_sim::script::consist`) puts each cable's sent value on the line and delivers the largest to every unit of the connected run (semantics in the [runtime sheet](../../architecture/osc-runtime.md)). `[couple_front]` is not needed: the consist plan already gives the order.
- [x] `[scriptshare]` resolution: a unit without its own engine reads the engine of the nearest unit before it that owns a store (animations, sounds, doors).
- [x] Trigger routing: keys and protocol triggers go to the head's engine only (a car behind with a cab of its own would answer the same key and switch the head off); hot-spot clicks go to the part whose mesh was hit (`MouseTrigger.owner`); scripted clicks to the first engine with the trigger.
- [ ] One writer for `articulation_*_alpha/beta/gamma` (remove the duplicate in rail and ground code).
- [x] Rail: the vehicles' own `M_Wheel`/`Brakeforce` replace the assumed `RailDynamics` constants (built-in model as fallback); wheel radius assumed 0.33 m.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. NG272 (GN92, the reference vehicle, articulated): the rear doors open from their own hot-spots and keys; indicator and door state agree across parts.
2. NF6D: every declared cable pair is copied (verified in the rig); the 23-part double traction runs at 1 ms per step or less.
3. Rail golden run: 0-50 km/h and service braking within the published order of magnitude for the type ❓.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- Order of the cable copy relative to the parts' `{frame}` runs is unverified (Q-VS-1): test both orders in the rig.
- Shared stores must not change script semantics: keep a per-part view of private names.
