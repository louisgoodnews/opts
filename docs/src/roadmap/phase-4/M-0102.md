## 🎯 Objective

The vehicle's whole sound set follows the scripts' events and `Snd_*` variables; announcements play; force-feedback variables reach a controller when present.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** `sound.cfg` parser, event bus, mixer binding, announcements, `FF_*`.

**Out of scope:** The audio engine itself (M-0027), music, radio content.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [x] `sound.cfg` parser (`opts_core::formats::sound`, M-0139) with `[trigger]`, loops and volume curves, bound to the vehicle's script variables (`player_sound.rs`, `sound_logic.rs`; census and the condition modes in [sound_cfg](../../formats/text/vehicles/sound_cfg.md)).
- [~] Script events reach the sound layer (`VehicleScripts::raised`, played as one-shots); every part's engine (trailers: done 2026-10-20 for the units' `[sound]`); open: the payload of `(T.F)` events (announcement files). Original text: the audio layer subscribes; payload of `(T.F)` events (announcement files).
- [ ] `Snd_*` variables (`Snd_OutsideVol`, `Snd_Microphone`, `Snd_Radio`, …) as mixer inputs.
- [ ] `FF_Vib_*` to a controller abstraction (no-op without a device).

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. Every `[trigger]` of NG272, SD200 and HH109 is reached by an event in a scripted run; at least 90 % of all `sound.cfg` triggers of the corpus match an event that their set raises.
2. No event is dropped silently: the report lists events without a sound mapping.
3. Engine-raised events (Q-BSI-4) are listed and stubbed explicitly.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- Depends on the audio engine of M-0027; until then the phase delivers the event stream and the parser behind a no-op mixer.
- **Audio crate (decided 2026-10-14):** design against the capabilities of `bevy_kira_audio` and do not add the dependency until this phase starts. Requirements: 3D spatial audio per source, runtime volume and pitch modulation, conditional gating (`[conditionSingle]`), dozens of simultaneous sounds per vehicle, inside/outside viewpoint scoping. Checked 2026-10-14: version 0.27.1 declares Bevy 0.20, needs Rust 1.96, default features `ogg` and `spatial`, OMSI's `.wav` needs the `wav` feature. At the start: re-confirm the version match and evaluate Bevy's own audio rewrite; if it covers the requirements, Kira is optional.
