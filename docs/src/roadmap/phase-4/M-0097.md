## 🎯 Objective

The script's `M_Wheel`, `Brakeforce`, `Axle_Brakeforce_*` and `Axle_Springfactor_*` drive a per-wheel physics model that bounds them by friction and returns wheel speeds to the script; script drive becomes the default for vehicles whose scripts write `M_Wheel`.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** Wheel model, torque and brake distribution, suspension stiffness, resistances, spring-brake hold, calibration of the `M_Wheel` unit, removal of `--script-drive`.

**Out of scope:** Rail dynamics (M-0098), consist mass (M-0053), tyre-slip detail beyond a friction circle.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [ ] Per-wheel angular state in `opts-sim` (inertia, torque, brake, slip limited by a friction circle with simple load transfer); `Wheel_RotationSpeed_*`, `n_Wheel`, `Velocity_Ground` produced by it.
- [ ] `M_Wheel` distributed over the driven axles of `[newachse]`; per-wheel or total brake force; `Axle_Springfactor_*` into the suspension model.
- [ ] Rolling resistance from `[rollwiderstand]`, drag, grade from the terrain; hold on grades with the spring brake; fix of the backward-roll hazard (zero-speed guard uses the script's brake, not the driver's pedal).
- [ ] `M_WHEEL_UNIT` constant calibrated on three vehicles of known mass (experiment E1 of the strategy).
- [ ] Script drive default with the three-way fallback of acceptance criterion 5; the start-up emulation becomes an optional "cockpit procedure" that the player can switch off; fallback to the built-in drivetrain when no script writes `M_Wheel`.
- [ ] Tests: plausibility suite (below), step-size independence.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. Verified in the rig first, then in the game window: SD200 (solo bus) reaches 50 km/h from standstill in 8-14 s and stops from 50 km/h in 12-20 m at full brake pedal; the reference vehicle NG272 (GN92, articulated) lies in the range fixed by the E1 calibration and is then ratcheted as its golden run; holds on a 10 % grade with the parking brake and does not creep backward.
2. A throttle sweep gives monotone acceleration; no script output can produce a force above the friction limit.
3. Results at 30, 60 and 120 Hz physics step agree within 5 %.
4. Golden CSV of NG272 (primary), SD200, HH109 and one AI car reproduced.
5. **Fallback rule exercised by tests** (decided 2026-10-14): a script with non-zero torque on the drive axle is used; a script with zero torque falls back to the const-file torque curve (if present) with one `INFO` per vehicle; a vehicle with neither falls back to the constants model with one `WARN` per vehicle; script drive is not gated on all callbacks being implemented. Tested with a broken script, a zero-torque script and a vehicle without scripts.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- Unit and distribution of `M_Wheel` unverified: calibration constant, friction clamp, plausibility tests.
- The built-in drivetrain remains for vehicles without working scripts; both paths are tested.
