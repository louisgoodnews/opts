## 🎯 Objective

AI vehicles near the player run their small scripts (`{frame}` and `{frame_ai}`) with the pedals the traffic planner writes, through the same physics contract; distant ones keep the kinematic model.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** Script execution for AI vehicles, LOD switch, use of `AiResponse`.

**Out of scope:** Scheduled AI, new IDM behaviour.

---

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

- [~] *(status sync 2026-10-25: done for scheduled AI vehicles (`ai_script.rs`: `AI`, `AI_Engine`, `AI_Scheduled_AtStation`, `PAX_*_Req`, `ai_scheduled_settarget`); the planner's pedals do not drive the scripts' physics.)* Scripts of AI vehicles loaded with the AI flags; `{frame_ai}` blocks run where present; planner `Throttle`/`Brake`/indicators written like OMSI's engine-side AI.
- [~] *(status sync 2026-10-25: the 150 m script radius exists (`SCRIPT_RADIUS_M`); no wheel model or hand-over, the vehicles stay kinematic.)* LOD: scripts and the wheel model within a radius (default 150 m), kinematic beyond; state hand-over without a speed jump.
- [ ] `AiResponse` (M-0095 follow-up) as the cheap kinematic acceleration model, validated against the scripted response.
- [ ] Input completeness for lorry and police scripts (the 85 deviating and 23 inert vehicles of the AI response census) from the input table of M-0096.

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. 40 near vehicles at most 3 ms per step; no speed change above 0.3 m/s at the LOD switch.
2. Scripted and kinematic acceleration of the same vehicle agree within 15 %.
3. The AI response census reports at most 5 inert road vehicles.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

- Per-vehicle script cost times 40: budget enforced by the radius.
