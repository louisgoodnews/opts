## 🎯 Objective

AI vehicles near the player run their small scripts (`{frame}` and `{frame_ai}`) with the pedals the traffic planner writes, through the same physics contract; distant ones keep the kinematic model.

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** Script execution for AI vehicles, LOD switch, use of `AiResponse`.

**Out of scope:** Scheduled AI, new IDM behaviour.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. 40 near vehicles at most 3 ms per step; no speed change above 0.3 m/s at the LOD switch.
2. Scripted and kinematic acceleration of the same vehicle agree within 15 %.
3. The AI response census reports at most 5 inert road vehicles.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

## 🧭 Design for the near-vehicle scripts (2026-11-25)

Not yet built. What the code shows and what the change needs:

- **Where accelerations are set.** Every traffic agent takes its acceleration in one place, `crates/opts-traffic/src/agent/step/accel.rs` (`acc = p.acceleration(...)`, then `integrate_agents`). The game steps the world in `crates/opts-game/src/in_game/omsi_world/traffic/motion.rs` (`move_traffic`) and places the models from the agent pose.
- **Proposed split.** Each frame, for agents within `SCRIPT_RADIUS_M` of the camera and on the fallback list: the game turns the planner's desired acceleration into `Throttle`/`Brake` (the same mapping as the shared car law), runs the vehicle's `{frame}` blocks, reads `M_Wheel` and `Brakeforce`, and converts them to an acceleration with the same law as `opts-kinematic`. The traffic world takes that value in place of the IDM result for those agents only. A new pub hook in `opts-traffic` (`set_external_accel`) is the only change in that crate.
- **Hand-over.** The speed is continuous by construction (the agent keeps its speed; only its acceleration source changes), so the 0.3 m/s criterion is tested on the speed trace, not assumed.
- **Budgets.** `opts-game` (90,578 lines) and `opts-traffic` (6,142) are both at their budget. The hook and the per-frame mapping need room: a budget raise (owner) or code moved out, as with the earlier splits.
- **Verification.** Acceptance 1 needs an in-game run (40 near vehicles, timed per step, and the speed trace across the switch) under the shared game lock. Acceptance 2 is the agreement check already in place; the fallback list is what the gate reads.

Built 2026-11-25 without budget raises (crate splits: `opts-vehicle-traits`, `opts-signals`, `opts-traffic-weather`, `opts-stream-budget`, and the pure `opts-scripted-drive`). The game-run slot is still needed for criterion 1.
