## 🎯 Objective

The mini map shows the course of the selected timetable as a line on the roads and tells where on it the vehicle is, using only the paths of the map; no navigation, no route search, no turn-by-turn.

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** `StnLinks.cfg` and `.ttr` parsing; a map-wide element index; the course resolver; vehicle-to-lane matching; the mini map's course stroke.

**Out of scope:** the `.hof` and timetable model (M-0099), route search and re-routing, AI trains and scheduled AI buses, any overlay in the 3D world, text instructions.

## 📦 Deliverables & Tasks

1. [ ] **Corpus check of the entry fields** (an `opts-core` example): third field = path number, second = direction; counts per map; H1 (consecutive entries are graph successors) measured as a join rate.
2. [ ] **Parsers** `StnLinks.cfg` and `.ttr` in `opts_core::formats::map::timetable` with self-authored fixtures and a corpus test.
3. [ ] **Element index** `ID code -> (tile, element kind)` for the whole map, built lazily and cached per session (tile radius does not limit it).
4. [ ] **`Course` resolver**: `Vec<CourseLane { source, number, reversed }>` from a trip; lanes looked up by `Lane.key`; unresolved entries reported, never silently dropped.
5. [ ] **`LaneGraph::lanes_near` and `locate`** with hysteresis and course bonus; rail kind supported (a rail graph or a kind filter in place of the road-only `retain`).
6. [ ] **Mini map**: `MapRoad` carries `on_course`/`ahead`; one more stroke style from the instrument tokens; a `hud.minimap` field for the matched lane and course position.
7. [ ] **Docs and verification**: a bus on a real course (Type 2) and a tram (Type 1) with screenshots; the OMSI question "what happens off course" added to the experiment protocol.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. On three maps with station links, at least 99 % of the entries of every trip resolve to lanes, and at least 95 % of consecutive entries are successors in the graph (otherwise the milestone reports the cause per map rather than hiding it).
2. Driving a course on a reference map keeps the match on the correct lane at junctions and parallel lanes (no flicker: at most one lane change of the match per genuine lane change), and a match update stays within 0.2 ms.
3. The mini map draws the course in a distinct, token-based stroke; with no course the map is unchanged.
4. All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no OMSI asset committed.

## ⚠️ Risks

- The meaning of the entry fields is unverified (Q-T04); step 1 exists to settle it before anything depends on it.
- A course crosses tiles that are not resident; the element index must not force tile loading.
- Chrono overlays change tiles and `TTData` by date; the index and the course must be built from the active chrono state.
- Rail and road lanes meet at level crossings and shared tracks; matching must be kind-aware.
