## 🎯 Objective

Let the player choose a vehicle's repaint (`.cti` files in the `[CTC]` folders) and see it in the game: texture slots (`[CTCTexture]`) are replaced, and the repaint's `[setvar]` equipment options are written into the vehicle's script variables.

This milestone was listed in the roadmap but had no file. The work below shipped in Phase 4 without one; this file records it after the fact (F-022).
