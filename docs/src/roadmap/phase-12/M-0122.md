## 🎯 Objective

Bring the in-game picture close to the reference characteristics of the project lead's screenshot (crisp sun shadows, sharp anisotropically filtered ground, glossy paint, glass that reflects yet shows the interior, subtle ambient occlusion, clean edges, restrained bloom, filmic tonemapping) at a frame-time budget that holds on an RTX 3060 12 GB at 1920x1080, with every effect behind a setting and the existing quality ladder.

**What exists already (build on it, do not duplicate), verified 2026-10-18:**

- HDR cameras and tonemapping with three looks (Vivid default, Natural, Bevy): `crates/opts-game/src/look.rs` ([M-0060](../phase-8/M-0060.md) step 1 done; calibration open). MSAA 4x on the main camera through Bevy's default (no explicit `Msaa` setting in `opts-game`/`opts-bevy`, and no user setting; [texture quality](../../architecture/texture-quality.md) says "keep MSAA 4x"); mirror and reflection cameras use `Msaa::Off` (`crates/opts-bevy/src/mirrors.rs`, `reflections.rs`). Cascaded shadows, light shafts, cones and the LightQuality ladder with the frame-time governor ([lighting system](../../architecture/lighting-system.md), `crates/opts-bevy/src/light_quality.rs`). Texture filtering up to 16x and mip chains ([M-0057](../phase-8/M-0057.md), [M-0058](../phase-8/M-0058.md) partly done). Wet ground, reflections prototype, weather ([weather](../../architecture/weather.md), [camera system](../../architecture/camera-system.md), [texture quality](../../architecture/texture-quality.md)).
- **Missing in the game:** ambient occlusion, TAA, SMAA/FXAA and bloom. A grep over `crates/` finds Bevy's `Bloom` only in `crates/opts-bevy/examples/lighting_bench.rs`; `LightQuality.bloom` and the setting `graphics.bloom` are plumbing that no camera consumes (off in every ladder row). The reflections research notes that SSAO needs `Msaa::Off` plus depth and normal prepass ([reflections research](../../architecture/reflections-research.md)); TAA also needs `Msaa::Off`.
- Tools: `opts-tools image-metrics`, `scripts/visual-ab.sh`, `scripts/visual-shot.sh` ([visual A/B](../../architecture/visual-ab.md)) and the lighting bench example with `msaa`, `hdr`, `bloom` keys.

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** (a) **PBR audit**: how OMSI materials (diffuse, `[matl_envmap]`, specular and shininess keys, `[matl_alpha]`) map to `StandardMaterial` metallic, roughness, reflectance; fix systematic errors (paint too matte or too plastic, rubber and trim too glossy, glass); (b) **ambient occlusion**: evaluate Bevy's SSAO (and a cheap baked or vertex alternative for interiors) and ship the best value; (c) **anti-aliasing decision** between MSAA 4x (today), SMAA, TAA (with its prepass, motion-vector and shimmer-on-camera-shake costs); (d) **bloom**: connect the existing `graphics.bloom` plumbing to a restrained Bloom on the main camera, off in the lower ladder rungs; (e) **texture detail**: measure and tune sharpness (anisotropy, mip bias) without redoing M-0057/M-0058; (f) **benchmark and budget** on the reference hardware; (g) settings, ladder integration, docs.

**Out of scope:** shadows (M-0091), the sun and exposure model (M-0060), mip generation and caches (M-0058), foliage mips (M-0059), ray tracing, DLSS/FSR upscalers (see [bevy-0-20-dlss](../../architecture/bevy-0-20-dlss.md)), volumetric clouds, new models or textures.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. **Benchmark:** on the RTX 3060 12 GB at 1920x1080, the reference bus-stop scene at the default (Automatic) quality holds a 16.7 ms average with the 99th-percentile frame under 25 ms (numbers set from a measured baseline before the first change and recorded; the milestone fails if the default setting regresses the baseline by more than 2 ms average without the project lead's approval).
2. **Ladder:** the frame-time governor, run on a deliberately overloaded scene, sheds the new effects in the documented order and recovers; a test on the ladder table covers each new row (`crates/opts-bevy/src/light_quality/tests.rs` pattern).
3. **A/B against the reference characteristics:** on the bus-stop and interior views the after-image compared with the before-image (a) darkens contact regions under the bus and in the interior (mean luma of two marked crops drops by the documented margin), (b) keeps ground high-frequency energy at or above the baseline Laplacian, (c) shows no bloom halo larger than the documented radius on non-emissive surfaces (crop test on a white sign), (d) keeps the darkest-pixel and contrast metrics inside the band approved by the project lead. The lead's visual sign-off is recorded.
4. **Anti-aliasing:** the chosen method reduces the edge-aliasing metric on a fixed edge crop (Laplacian energy of a high-contrast diagonal edge) versus Off, and cutout foliage still shows clean edges with M-0059's alpha-to-coverage path or a documented equivalent; no ghosting on a 5 m/s sideways camera pan above the documented limit if TAA is chosen.
5. **Settings:** every new effect has an Off value, a persisted setting, a `--`flag or control-protocol switch, and Off reproduces today's image (MAE below 0.5 %); defaults documented.
6. **Phase 8 untouched:** no open checkbox of M-0057..M-0060 or M-0091 is ticked or reworded by this milestone; shared decisions are linked.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset or screenshot committed.

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| SSAO and TAA require `Msaa::Off`, which conflicts with MSAA 4x and with alpha-to-coverage foliage ([M-0059](../phase-8/M-0059.md)) | Decide the AA mode first; measure SMAA/TAA against MSAA on foliage views; keep a path where AO is off when MSAA is on |
| Bevy 0.20 effect support or quality differs from expectations | Prototype in the lighting bench before touching the game; record versions |
| Frame-time budget exceeded on the reference GPU | Ladder placement and governor; an effect that cannot meet the budget ships off by default |
| TAA ghosting on the camera, mirrors and tram/train motion | Optional; SMAA fallback; reset on camera cut |
| "Looks like the screenshot" is subjective | Numbers on the existing metrics plus the lead's sign-off; no screenshot is committed |
| Scope creep into Phase 8 areas | The split table above; changes to those areas go to the Phase 8 milestones |
