## 🎯 Objective

A bus weighs what it carries: dynamic mass = curb weight + passengers x average passenger mass. A full bus accelerates more slowly, needs a longer braking distance, steers heavier and sits lower on its suspension than an empty one. The player can switch the effect off.

**What exists already (build on it, do not duplicate), verified 2026-10-18:**

- Vehicle mass is a constant today: the manifest's `[mass]` (`mass_tonnes`, `crates/opts-bevy/src/vehicle.rs`) feeds `VehicleSimulation::new_standard_bus(mass_kg, wheelbase_m)` (`crates/opts-bevy/src/vehicle_sim.rs`) and the drivetrain/brake model, where `vehicle_mass_kg` scales braking force and rolling resistance (`crates/opts-sim/src/vehicle/drivetrain.rs`). Consists sum the masses of their units ([vehicle physics](../../simulation/vehicle_physics.md), Consist Mass).
- [M-0073](../phase-10/M-0073.md) plans per-vehicle mass, axle loads, suspension and brakes and says "mass (empty + passengers later)" in its architecture task; its criteria test different vehicles, not different loads of one vehicle. **Gap:** it defines no dynamic-mass input, no per-axle load shift from passengers (position of the load along the bus) and no ride-height-from-load test.
- [M-0101](../phase-4/M-0101.md) is not started. It scopes `PAX_*` door/request variables, `humans_count` (417 uses, `L.L`; [predefined variables](../../formats/text/vehicles/predefined-variables.md)), `GetHumanCountOnSeat` and `GetHumanCountOnPathLink` (index space unverified, experiment E6 in the [experiment protocol](../../architecture/omsi-experiment-protocol.md); [OSC runtime](../../architecture/osc-runtime.md) says they return 0 today). **Gap:** it defines no total passenger count or mass output for the physics, and no per-zone distribution.
- Vehicle scripts do not carry mass of people; whether OMSI itself changes the vehicle's mass with passengers is ❓ (the audit checks, by experiment, whether any stock behaviour exists to match).

---

## 🔍 Scope

**In scope:** (a) a `PassengerLoad` input per vehicle unit: count, optional distribution (front/rear, per axle share) and an average passenger mass setting (default 75 kg, the documented source and the editable range recorded); (b) dynamic mass = curb weight + count x average, applied to every consumer of mass: acceleration, braking, rolling resistance, steering effort, and (with [M-0073](../phase-10/M-0073.md)) axle loads, suspension compression and ride height; (c) passenger count source: M-0101's passenger system (`humans_count`, `GetHumanCountOnSeat`, `PAX_*` door flow), with a **manual override** (control protocol and a debug-panel field) for testing before M-0101 exists; (d) articulated units: load per unit; (e) setting *Passenger weight*: Off / Realistic (plus the average mass field); (f) save and restore; (g) tests and docs.

**Out of scope:** the passenger system itself (M-0101), standing-passenger comfort, the sway of people, luggage, lateral load shifts from passenger movement, the vehicle solver (M-0073), passenger mass in AI traffic.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

1. `dynamic_mass_kg(curb 11,000 kg, 60, 75)` returns 15,500 kg; with the setting Off it returns 11,000 kg for any count (unit tests).
2. **Acceleration test:** the same bus, on the same flat road at full throttle, covers a shorter distance in 10 s when empty than when carrying its rated capacity at the default 75 kg; the difference is at least the documented margin (physics-derived from the mass ratio, tolerance 15 %).
3. **Braking test:** from 50 km/h with the same brake command the loaded bus's stopping distance is longer than the empty bus's by at least the documented margin, and by no more than the mass ratio allows; the empty case is unchanged from today's value with the setting Off.
4. Steering effort (the steer-axle load input) is higher when loaded; with [M-0073](../phase-10/M-0073.md) present, the rear axle load of a loaded articulated bus rises and its suspension ride height drops by an amount derived from the spring rate, within 15 % (test with documented values).
5. The manual override works with M-0101 absent; the source in use is shown (as `mass.source` in the `vehicle.passengers` answer, no debug panel exists); setting a count of 0 gives exactly the curb mass.
6. Saving and restoring keeps the count (the override; the people on board are not saved, see [session save](../../architecture/session-save.md)); the replay determinism tests are unchanged with the setting Off.

All gates pass (`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features`, `./scripts/local-ci.sh`); no unsafe code; no OMSI asset committed.

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| M-0073 and M-0101 are unfinished and their interfaces move | One adapter each; the interim total-mass path and the manual override make the milestone testable on its own |
| M-0101's passenger index space is inferred (E6) | Use the sum over indices behind one function; keep the override |
| Stock scripts already scale performance with passengers in some vehicles | The audit checks for double counting; where a script does it, document and avoid applying the load twice |
| Physical limits (grip, traction at full load) make a loaded bus unrealistic | Tests at documented tolerances; traction limits stay with M-0073 |
