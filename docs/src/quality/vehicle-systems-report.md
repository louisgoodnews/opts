# Vehicle Systems Report (M-0141)

*What the vehicles' own scripts do in OPTS, system by system, checked live on 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-21 (mostly the MAN New Lion's City 18C, the Bremen MAN NG313 and the Tram NF6D; probes are `vehicle.script_vars`, `vehicle.click`, `vehicle.key`, `vehicle.dynamics`, `vehicle.passengers` and screenshots). Detail pages: [air pressure](air-pressure-report.md), [start procedures](vehicle-start-procedures.md), [controls](controls-report.md), [passengers](../simulation/passengers.md).*

The principle: the `.osc` scripts run unchanged; the host feeds their inputs (`opts_bevy::vehicle_script`), takes what they write for the physics (torque, brake force, spring factors) and shows the rest through the model's animations and the sound events. A "system" works when its script runs *and* what it writes has an effect.

| System | Script runs | Effect in the game | Verified |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Electrics, ignition, engine start | yes | the battery key, the ignition and the starter bring the engine up from cold ([procedures](vehicle-start-procedures.md)) | NLC, NG313, Citybus, tram |
| Engine, gearbox | yes | `M_Wheel` drives the wheels; the automatic shifts | NLC, NG313, NG272, Urbino 12 (see below) |
| Service brake and parking brake | yes | per-wheel brake force from the script's own pressure model; 100 kN in all at full pedal on the NLC | NLC |
| Air pressure | yes | tanks, compressor with governor (cut-in 8.0, cut-out 9.5 bar), consumers: doors, kneeling, brakes ([report](air-pressure-report.md)) | NLC |
| Kneeling and raising | yes | **fixed 2026-10-20**: the script's spring factor now scales the whole spring, the NLC kneels on the kerb side (3.8 cm lower, 1.7° of roll) and rises again | NLC |
| Doors | yes | the leaves animate by door type (interior swing, outward swing, outward-swinging sliding; corrected 2026-10-20 in [animation axes](../architecture/animation-axes.md)), `PAX_*_Open` goes to the passengers, door sounds | NLC (all three types), NG313 |
| Door keys | yes | per vehicle kind: buses Num / and Num * (doors 1, 2), Ctrl+Num / and Ctrl+Num * (doors 3, 4), Num - clearance, Num + all doors (the NLC has no stadium switch); trams clearance and side toggle ([keys](../simulation/vehicle-keys.md)) | NLC, NF6D |

## Not working or not checked

* **Driver displays** (IBIS, matrix, printer, dashboard screens): the script textures belong to [M-0100](../roadmap/phase-4/M-0100.md).
* **Other vehicles** than the five above: only the controls census ([report](controls-report.md)) covers them.
* **Cash desk visuals**: coins at the change point, money from passengers on the desk, the driver's choice of ticket for a waiting passenger ([passengers](../simulation/passengers.md), open).
* **Trams**: since 2026-10-21 every car of the NF6D with scripts runs its own engine and the control cables copy the battery switch, indicators, door commands and traction demand between them ([M-0098](../roadmap/phase-4/M-0098.md)); the doors of cars A, B and C open from the cab's commands and the passengers board through each (see [Passengers](../simulation/passengers.md)). The cab has to be activated (the combined switch to N) before clearance works, as in the original; the cars' drive torque still comes from the built-in rail model, not their scripts.
* **Urbino 12 (Solaris Urbino II, `SU_12.bus`)** looked as if it did not move in the sound harness; two host faults were behind it, **fixed 2026-10-21**, and its own scripts now drive it. Its parking brake is a spring brake that releases only when both supply circuits (`Vorratsdruck_Kreis1/2`, filled by the engine at `engine_n / 4000` bar per second) are above 5.6 bar, and while it is applied the gearbox script goes to neutral at standstill (`antrieb_neutral_requested`). (1) With no script torque the host fell back to its built-in drivetrain, which drove the bus through the applied spring brake and hid the interlock: the fallback is now off while the script holds its parking brake (`bremse_feststell`). (2) The host toggled the script's parking brake after what `bremse_feststell` showed, which also reads "applied" while the air is down, so the toggle flipped the lever to applied for good: a vehicle whose lever is `cockpit_parking_brake` (1 means released) now gets the idempotent `parking_brake_set` / `parking_brake_release`. Live: the circuits pass 5.6 bar after about five seconds, the brake releases, the gears shift 1 to 4 under the script's `M_Wheel` (5,900 down to 1,850 N·m), 46 km/h after 33 s at 60 % throttle. The NLC (toggle) and the NG313 were re-checked and drive as before; the start-procedure tests pass.
* **`{if}` and the stack.** The NLC's own `parking_brake_set` and `parking_brake_release` are written as `(L.L.bremse_feststell) {if} ! (S.L.bremse_feststell)`, which only makes sense when the value tested stays on the stack after `{if}`; the VM (and the [osc spec](../formats/text/vehicles/osc.md)) pop it, so on the NLC these two triggers store the wrong value (`release` leaves the brake applied) and the host toggles instead. Whether OMSI's `{if}` keeps the value is not settled; the host works around it.
