Every irregularity the offline detectors report is catalogued here. `Status`: *fixed* = OPTS bug repaired with a regression test; *tolerance* = deterministic, documented handling of broken content; *content defect* = the source asset is broken and OPTS draws what it can; *benign* / *informational* = the detector fires on legitimate content; *open* = a real defect without a fix.

## How well do the detectors find wrongly decoded meshes?

Measured on plain/keyed twin pairs (ground truth): a decoded mesh is *wrong* when fewer than 98 % of its vertices match the twin.

```
agreement 0.90..0.92: accepted 72, wrong 41 (57 %)
agreement 0.92..0.94: accepted 59, wrong 41 (69 %)
agreement 0.94..0.96: accepted 63, wrong 33 (52 %)
agreement 0.96..0.98: accepted 90, wrong 44 (49 %)
agreement 0.98..1.00: accepted 161, wrong 19 (12 %)
agreement 1.00..1.02: accepted 1381, wrong 123 (9 %)
long-edge rule > 6x median: dropped bad 8087 good 19707 (of 588662 faces)
long-edge rule > 10x median: dropped bad 5535 good 8920 (of 588662 faces)
long-edge rule > 15x median: dropped bad 3385 good 4679 (of 588662 faces)
long-edge rule > 25x median: dropped bad 1178 good 1617 (of 588662 faces)
long-edge rule > 40x median: dropped bad 97 good 148 (of 588662 faces)
twin pairs 1861 accepted 1826 wrong(<98% positions) 301
code	flagged_wrong	flagged_right	recall	precision
ANY	32	172	0.11	0.16
DEGENERATE_TRIS	3	0	0.01	1.00
HUGE_TRIS	14	2	0.05	0.88
OUTLIER_VERTS	3	0	0.01	1.00
UV_CHAOS	12	170	0.04	0.07
```

Reading: *agreement* is the two annealing runs' share of identical vertex labels; below 0.98 about half of the accepted meshes are wrong, above it about one in ten. The `long-edge rule` lines count the faces longer than k times the median edge that are **still present after** `drop_spike_faces` ran (40x is the rule that is applied; before it existed the same measurement gave 10,373 wrong / 20,169 correct faces beyond 6x, 7,812 / 9,037 beyond 10x, 3,414 / 2,343 beyond 25x and 2,303 / 1,049 beyond 40x, which is why 40x was chosen). The table of detector codes (computed on the filtered decodes, codes that the plain twin itself triggers excluded) shows that the generic geometric detectors find only about one in ten wrongly decoded meshes (recall 0.11) and that texture-coordinate chaos is useless as a signal (precision 0.07): without a plain twin a wrongly decoded mesh is mostly **not** detectable by geometry alone, the agreement is the best available indicator.
