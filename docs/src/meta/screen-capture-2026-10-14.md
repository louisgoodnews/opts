## Design summary

Capture reads the application window's own render target (`Screenshot::window`), never the desktop, so it is window-only in windowed and fullscreen mode alike. Commands: an extended `screenshot`, `capture.burst`, `capture.record.start|stop|status` and `capture.prune`. Recordings are off unless started, bounded by duration (default 60 s, cap 30 min), size (512 MB, cap 2 GB) and free disk (1 GiB), run on an encoder thread behind a bounded queue and degrade by dropping frames. Every capture has metadata (simulation time, frame, map, tile, camera, vehicle, window) returned and written as a sidecar. Video is AV1 in IVF through the `rav1e` already in the build; bursts are PNG sequences; H.264/MP4 is a possible optional feature (ADR-0012). Files go only to `<user data dir>/opts/captures`; the new commands take relative names.

## Implementation summary

`crates/opts-bevy/src/capture/` (storage, metadata, screenshot, backend, jobs, burst, recording, worker, record_cmd, encoder, av1, ivf, prune), the `screenshot` handler delegating to it, CLI specs and spaced command names (`opts-cli capture record start`), a game-side metadata hook, `scripts/capture-agent-demo.sh`. Two direct dependencies were added to `opts-bevy`, both already in the build: `rav1e` (same feature set as through `image`) and `sysinfo` (disk feature).

## Performance measurements

Main menu only, debug build, 1920x1013 window; details and method in the design page, "Measurements".

* Game frame rate during a recording, **menu only (debug build)**: 85-89 % of the frame rate before. **On a loaded scene (release build, Waldhofen, NG272, AI traffic): 97-99 % at the defaults, 97 % in the worst case** (full size at 60 fps requested); bar: 80 %. Table and method in the design page.
* Capture rate at 30 fps requested: 9 fps at full size, 23 fps at half size (the default), 29 fps at a quarter size. The encoder is the limit; frames it cannot take are dropped and counted.
* Readback about 6 ms per frame and asynchronous. Stage timings are in `capture.record.status` (`timing_ms`).

## Test results

* `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features` and `./scripts/local-ci.sh` pass.
* New tests: path traversal (including symlinks), parameter validation, PNG dimensions, sidecar content, burst count and spacing, IVF well-formedness and frame count (also with `ffprobe`), remux to MP4, MKV and WebM with `ffmpeg -c copy`, duration, size and disk limits, refusal on a nearly full disk, one job at a time, dropped frames on a stalled renderer, degradation on a low frame rate, presentation times with gaps, prune candidates.
* Live, in the main menu: screenshot (window content only, with metadata), burst of 5 and 10 frames, recordings of 3-8 s that probe as AV1 with the sidecar's frame count, `scripts/capture-agent-demo.sh` end to end (record, key presses, burst, screenshot, verification of every output).

## Deviations from the design

See the design page: burst length capped at 90 s total, `h264` not built, no 20 GB soft limit on the captures directory, no `markers` metadata, `capture.prune` added, `screenshot.path` restricted to the captures directory. New in phase C: **`scale` defaults to 0.5** (was 1.0) because of the measurements, and IVF timestamps follow the capture clock.

## Remaining issues

1. **Loaded-scene frame-rate criterion: passed** (2026-10-14, see the design page). Not done: recording while the vehicle drives, and a machine with few cores.
2. Occluded and minimized windows, fullscreen and Wayland frame callbacks are unverified on a real window (the code paths exist and are unit tested with a synthetic backend).
3. Full-size 30 fps is not reachable with the dependency-free encoder (see the design page).
4. `docs/book/` (generated by `local-ci.sh`) is not in `.gitignore`; it was committed by accident once on this branch and removed from the unpushed history.
