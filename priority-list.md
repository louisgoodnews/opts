## 🧠 Prioritization Rationale & Scoring Methodology

With **73 open milestones** spanning 15 phases, executing milestones in chronological ID order (`M-0001` $\to$ `M-0150`) would interleave nearly-finished cockpit work with multi-month editor suites and unguarded code growth. Instead, this priority list ranks every open roadmap item using **five explicit engineering criteria**:

1. **Compound Leverage & Guardrails First (Multiplier Effect):**
   Tooling and pre-commit gates that reduce the cost, defect rate, or AI token footprint of *every subsequent commit* ([`M-0147`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-15/M-0147.md) structure/token ratchets, [`M-0148`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-15/M-0148.md) inline test extraction, [`M-0042`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-5/M-0042.md)/[`M-0052`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-5/M-0052.md) CI proofs) rank at the very top. Landing guardrails first prevents new violations from accumulating while feature work proceeds.
2. **WIP Liquidation & "Last-5%" Closure (Zero Context-Switch Waste):**
   Several high-value milestones are **85–99% implemented** on `master` ([`M-0141`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0141.md), [`M-0100`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0100.md), [`M-0066`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-10/M-0066.md), [`M-0058`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-8/M-0058.md), [`M-0062`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-9/M-0062.md), [`M-0097`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0097.md), [`M-0109`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-11/M-0109.md), [`M-0124`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-12/M-0124.md), [`M-0113`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-11/M-0113.md), [`M-0110`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-11/M-0110.md), [`M-0077`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-10/M-0077.md)). Finishing these small remaining slices clears the [`BOARD.md`](docs/dev/BOARD.md) *In Progress* and *Review* queues and turns partial investments into closed milestones.
3. **Core Player Loop Completeness (Drive $\to$ Stops $\to$ Passengers $\to$ Audio $\to$ World):**
   OPTS's primary user experience is driving an OMSI 2 vehicle on a scheduled line. Anything that breaks or completes that core loop—cockpit displays and ticket printers ([`M-0141`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0141.md)/[`M-0100`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0100.md)), passenger cash desk ([`M-0101`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0101.md)), map terrain/curb defects ([`M-0090`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-10/M-0090.md)), vehicle/AI/scenery audio ([`M-0102`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0102.md)/[`M-0139`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0139.md)/[`M-0140`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0140.md)), and AI road/pedestrian interactions ([`M-0111`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-11/M-0111.md)/[`M-0049`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0049.md)/[`M-0050`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0050.md))—outranks secondary tooling or visual embellishments.
4. **Architectural Decoupling Before Feature Multiplication:**
   Splitting oversized files/functions ([`M-0149`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-15/M-0149.md), [`M-0150`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-15/M-0150.md)), extracting the world loader from `opts-game` into `opts-bevy` ([`M-0046`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0046.md)/[`M-0024`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-4/M-0024.md)), and migrating engine crates off the `opts-core` OMSI facade to native `opts-model` ([Phase 13](docs/src/roadmap/phase-13/README.md)) must happen **before** building large new editors or modding layers ([Phase 14](docs/src/roadmap/phase-14/README.md), [Phase 6](docs/src/roadmap/phase-6/README.md)) so new features do not deepen legacy coupling.
5. **Strict Dependency & Spec-First Ordering:**
   In accordance with OPTS's *Documentation-Driven Development* philosophy, research and design-checkpoint milestones ([`M-0135`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-14/M-0135.md), [`M-0118`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-11/M-0118.md), [`M-0116`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-11/M-0116.md)) are scheduled ahead of their implementation milestones ([`M-0136`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-14/M-0136.md)/[`M-0137`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-14/M-0137.md), [`M-0115`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-11/M-0115.md), [`M-0117`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-11/M-0117.md)), and release packaging ([`M-0034`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-7/README.md), [`M-0036`](docs/src/roadmap/phase-7/README.md)) sits at the end of the pipeline.
